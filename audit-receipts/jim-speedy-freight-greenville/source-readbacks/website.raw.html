    Speedy Freight FAQ

    • What logistics services do you offer?

      At Speedy Freight, we offer a comprehensive range of logistics services to meet your business needs. Our offerings include full truck load shipping, storage solutions, ecommerce fulfillment, and complete 3PL services. Whether you require efficient transportation, secure storage, or end-to-end logistics management, we’ve got you covered.

    • How do I book a delivery with Speedy Freight?

      Booking a delivery with Speedy Freight is easy and hassle-free. You can initiate the process by reaching out to our team by giving us a call, email, or simply filling out our web form. Once you’ve spoken with a member of our friendly team and completed the information about what you need us to ship, we’ll sort the rest.

    • How much notice do you need?

      We understand that logistics needs can arise suddenly, and we strive to be as flexible as possible. While we welcome advanced bookings, we are also equipped to accommodate last-minute requests. Get in touch with our team, and we’ll get your logistics needs sorted.

    • What can you transport?

      At Speedy Freight, we have the capability to transport a wide range of goods across various industries. We can handle shipments of all sizes and types. Whether it’s perishable goods, machinery, electronics, or just about anything in between, our experienced team is prepared to handle the transportation of your goods with utmost care and professionalism.

    • Can you deliver anywhere in the USA?

      Absolutely! Our logistics services cover the entirety of the United States. No matter where your business operates or where your shipment needs to go, you can rely on Speedy Freight to deliver promptly and efficiently, from coast to coast.
