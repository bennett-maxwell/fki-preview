## THE 6 AI AGENTS FOR DOE'S SPORTING GOODS

### Agent 1: Abandoned Cart Recovery Agent
This agent fires within 60 minutes of cart abandonment through your Shopify webhook. It sends a personalized SMS and email that mentions the specific gear the customer left behind — not a generic "you forgot something" message. For high-value carts over $200, it escalates to your team for personal follow-up. For carts under $50, it sends one SMS and stops. It logs every send and response to Google Sheets automatically.

Expected result: 15-20% cart recovery rate. At 120 monthly visitors and a $350 average ticket, recovering even 10 abandoned carts per month at that value adds $3,500 in monthly revenue that currently goes to zero.

### Agent 2: Loyalty Program Agent
This agent tracks purchase history in Square, auto-assigns loyalty tiers, and sends win-back sequences at 30, 60, and 90 days of inactivity. It handles birthday offers and milestone rewards automatically. Three tiers: Bronze for customers spending under $500 annually, Silver for $500-$999, Gold for $1,000 and above. Each tier gets different re-engagement sequences, different offers, and different communication frequency — all running without any staff time.

### Agent 3: Inventory Alert Agent
Every morning at 6am, this agent pulls Shopify inventory levels for every SKU, flags anything below reorder thresholds, checks QuickBooks for outstanding purchase orders, and generates a reorder recommendation for anything that needs attention. Every Monday at 7am, you get a full buying report: top 10 fastest-moving items, top 10 by revenue, bottom 10 by velocity flagged for potential markdown, and seasonal trend comparison. Delivered to your Gmail with a Google Sheets link for the full data.

### Agent 4: Review and Referral Agent
36 hours after every Square transaction over $25, this agent sends a personalized review request via SMS. It references the specific item purchased — not a generic "how was your experience" message. Positive replies get routed immediately to Google Review. Negative replies go to your inbox within 10 minutes for quick resolution. No review within 5 days gets a single email follow-up. Customers who opt out are never contacted again.

### Agent 5: Customer Win-Back Agent
This agent identifies customers who haven't purchased in 90 or more days from your Square data and runs personalized re-engagement sequences tied to their purchase history and category preferences. Day 30 gets a "here's what's new in your category" message. Day 60 gets a $15 credit with no expiration. Day 90 gets the best offer — 20% off any single item, valid for two weeks.

### Agent 6: In-Store Upsell Agent
Every morning, this agent analyzes past purchase patterns to identify the next-best product recommendation for each customer scheduled to come in or recently contacted. Your team gets a Gmail briefing that arms them with context before high-value customer interactions — what the customer bought last time, what category they shop most, and what complementary products make sense to mention. Zero research time for your staff; they walk into every customer interaction prepared.

## WHAT TO SKIP FOR NOW

John, AI can eventually handle almost anything a person does on a computer given the right access and training. The plan is to replace that work step by step — biggest wins first. These aren't things AI can't do. They're just not where we start for a $1.2M sporting goods store.

**Custom AI Website Chatbot:** Worth doing eventually, but requires significant training data and would distract from the abandoned cart and loyalty wins that compound fastest at your scale.

**AI-Generated Social Media Content:** Natural next step — but local retail wins more from Google reviews and loyalty reactivation than social posts at this stage. Month 2 territory.

**Predictive Demand Forecasting:** Powerful at scale. At $1.2M you don't yet have the data volume for the predictions to be meaningfully accurate. The inventory alert agent gets you 80% of the value with 10% of the complexity.

**AI-Powered Hiring and HR:** A natural build as you scale past $2M. For now, that's a future-state system — not a distraction from the revenue and time-recovery wins available today.
