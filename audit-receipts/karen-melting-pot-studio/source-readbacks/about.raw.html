Karen Farah

Karen began her journey in entrepreneurship with a successful construction-tech venture exit. As a Harvard MBA and with a Civil Engineering degree, she has held executive roles at Google, Tesla, Spotify, LinkedIn, and LiveNation, An expert in AI, Holistic Marketing, Advertising, Operations and overall Business Growth, she has served as COO and CPO in diverse industries including healthcare, construction, manufacturing, advertising, technology, finance, SaaS, and real estate. Forbes 30 Under 30 (AI & Science), Microsoft Top Leader of 2025, Great Companies Award Winner 2025, Global AI Sustainability Award winner, and NextGen Hispanic Executive 30 Under 30 honoree. Her portfolio includes landmark projects for Disney, NASA, and Hasbro. A founding member of NYC Women in STEM for Gender Equality, Karen is a passionate advocate for diversity in tech and continues to empower organizations worldwide through cutting-edge AI innovation and inclusive leadership.

Rob Young

With a rich background in entertainment, starting at Roc-A-Fella Records in its early days, Rob has steered global brands towards impactful partnerships and projects. From his work with Pepsi and the NBA to initiatives like Van Jones’ #GreenForAll, he merges branding and digital expertise with social good. Rob spearheads transformative initiatives, harnessing the power of influence to drive positive and meaningful change in organizations.

Karina Castolo

Karina is an AI Integration and Digital Transformation Strategist with experience spanning across enterprise consulting, international organizations, and government. Her work includes architecting agentic AI systems for the United Nations, founding Mexico's first Federal Judiciary Digital Transformation Unit, and co-instructing "Applied AI for Business Leaders" at Columbia University alongside other industry leaders. Through her independent consulting practice, she specializes in knowledge engineering, governance, and solution architecture, bridging the gap between what technology can do and what organizations are actually ready to implement. She holds an MS in Information and Knowledge Strategy from the University of Columbia. Karina's edge is seeing where technology, people, and organizational design meet and making sure the solution works for all three.
