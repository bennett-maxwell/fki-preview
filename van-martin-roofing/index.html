Ohio Roofing FAQ

Real questions from real Ohio homeowners.

What does a new roof cost in Ohio?

Most Ohio asphalt-shingle roof replacements run $9,000–$18,000 depending on home size, pitch, and material grade. Van Martin offers free in-home estimates and 0% financing.

Will my homeowners insurance pay for a storm-damaged roof?

In most cases, yes — if damage is from wind, hail, or a covered storm event. Van Martin's claim specialists meet your adjuster on-site to ensure the full scope is approved.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most single-family homes are done in 1 day. Larger or steeper roofs may take 2 days. We always tarp and clean up the same day.

Do you serve my Ohio city?

We have 9 offices across Ohio — Dayton, Cincinnati, Columbus, Springfield, Middletown, Hamilton, Lima, Mansfield, and Toledo. If you're within 60 miles of any of these, we cover you.

What warranty do you offer?

Lifetime workmanship warranty plus full manufacturer warranty (GAF Golden Pledge eligible). Transferable to the next homeowner.
