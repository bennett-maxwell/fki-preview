Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a custom pool cost in Southwest Florida?

Most Superior Pools custom builds in SWFL range from $65,000 to $180,000 depending on size, finish, screen enclosure, and outdoor living scope. Renovations typically run $18,000 to $55,000. We provide a fixed-price quote after the design consultation.

How long does a pool build take from contract to swim?

Most SWFL custom pools take 14 to 22 weeks from signed contract to first swim, including permitting, excavation, gunite cure, tile/finish, and final inspection. Renovations are typically 4 to 8 weeks.

Are you licensed and insured in Florida?

Yes. Superior Pools is fully licensed, bonded, and insured to build and renovate swimming pools throughout Florida. License number is displayed in the footer and on every signed contract.

Do you finance pool builds?

Yes. We partner with Lyon Financial and Synchrony for pool-specific financing — same-day pre-approval, no impact to your credit score to check your rate, and terms designed for the $60K–$200K range.

What warranty do you offer?

Superior Pools backs every new build with a structural warranty on the shell, a finish warranty on the interior surface, and the full manufacturer warranties on Hayward equipment. Specifics are itemized in your contract.

Do you build outside Fort Myers and Port Charlotte?

Yes — we serve the full Bradenton-to-Naples corridor including Cape Coral, Naples, Estero, Bonita Springs, Punta Gorda, Sarasota, Venice, Englewood and North Port.
