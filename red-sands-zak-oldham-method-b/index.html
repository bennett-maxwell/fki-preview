The Opportunity

Three places AI changes the economics of Red Sands

Red Sands receives ~85 kitchen design inquiries per month but only 22% convert. The bottleneck isn't lead volume — it's the 36-hour average response time on weekends and the 8 hours/week Zak personally spends on quoting. AI agents can compress that to under 5 minutes and free Zak for design work that closes deals.

01

Weekend response gap

36-hour avg response on weekend inquiries is bleeding ~6 qualified leads/month to competitors. AI intake closes the gap to under 5 minutes, 24/7.

02

Quoting bottleneck

Zak spends 8 hrs/week drafting estimates. Quote Forge cuts that to under 1 hour and lets Zak focus on the design phase that actually closes deals.

03

Cold-lead leakage

78% of inquiries don't convert in 30 days and never hear from Red Sands again. Sandstone Nurture re-engages them automatically — modeled to recover 8-12 deals/year.

Your AI Stack

Five agents built for how Red Sands actually operates

Red Sands runs HubSpot CRM, QuickBooks, and a Squarespace website. The AI stack overlays without replacement: a conversational lead-intake agent on the site, a quoting-assist agent in HubSpot, and a scheduling agent on email + SMS. Zero rip-and-replace.

01

Sandstone Intake

Greets every site visitor in under 3 seconds, captures kitchen scope (square footage, cabinets, timeline, budget band), qualifies against Red Sands fit criteria, and books the consult directly on Zak's calendar. Replaces the contact form.

Lead capture & qualification
02

Quote Forge

Pulls cabinet, counter, and labor cost tables; produces a first-draft estimate within 10 minutes of consult notes; routes to Zak for one-click approve / edit. Cuts quoting time from 8 hours/week to under 1.

Estimate drafting assistant
03

Reno Scheduler

Coordinates installer crews, material delivery windows, and homeowner availability via SMS. Resolves 80%+ of scheduling conflicts without Zak intervention.

Install coordination
04

Sandstone Nurture

Stays in touch with the 78% of leads who don't convert in the first 30 days. Sends design inspiration, project galleries, and seasonal pricing nudges. Re-engages cold leads automatically.

Long-cycle lead follow-up
05

Reputation Loop

Triggers 5 days after install completion, asks for a Google review, escalates dissatisfied responses directly to Zak, and posts five-star reviews to the Red Sands website carousel.

Post-install review capture
