the model

Sell to the School Once. Get Paid Every Season.

Your team travels to preschools, daycares and K-12 schools to photograph children during school hours. Proprietary EasyGo software runs everything — scheduling, parent communication, barcode photo matching, online ordering, lab fulfillment.

  • Schools book up to 4 sessions a year — spring, fall, cap-and-gown, holiday
  • SRP writes the school a commission check after every event
  • Why now: Lifetouch is losing school accounts — SRP franchisees are picking them up
  • ~7-month active season — summers and holidays off

One principal calls the team "the purple pep squad." Schools don't want to let them go.
