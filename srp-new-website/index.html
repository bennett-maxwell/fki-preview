Director & Parent FAQs

Common Questions Before You Book

The questions we hear most from directors and parents — straight answers, no fine print.

Be honest… how crazy is picture day going to be?

It's never a completely normal day, but it doesn't have to feel chaotic. We bring both a photographer and an assistant, so someone is always managing the line and keeping things moving while we handle communication and logistics.

What makes you different from every other photography company?

Our photographers are background checked and hired because they truly love working with kids — we've been called the "purple pep squad." We provide far more image variety and capture real personality, not one standard pose.

Are your prices higher than other companies?

Base pricing is very similar to competitors when families buy one or two images. The difference is we give families far more images to choose from and higher quality — more value, not more expensive.

What about shy kids… or the ones having a day?

We've met them all and genuinely enjoy them. We slow down, connect first, and read the room — no pressure, no forced grins, just authentic expressions.

How do you handle our families' data and images?

With a lot of care. We have a strict no sell, no share data policy — everything is stored on secure servers, and we never use an image without a signed model release.

Is this going to cost our school money?

No. There is no cost to your school. In fact, we pay a generous commission as a thank you for partnering with us, and teachers get a generous discount on their own orders.

Do you sell digital images, and can I order from my phone?

Yes — digital images are available for immediate download, with an email receipt and copyright release. Our mobile ordering system lets you tap an image, choose how you'd like it printed, and add it to your cart.

What should my child wear on picture day?

Solid colors or small prints, clothes your child feels comfortable in, and avoid bold patterns, stripes, large logos, or writing so the focus stays on their face.
