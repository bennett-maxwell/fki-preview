Investment and candidate fit

Know the requirements before you take the next step.

The strongest candidates combine curiosity, financial responsibility, a clean background, and a genuine desire to work with children and schools.

  • Access to approximately $50,000–$60,000 for startup
  • Credit score of at least 660
  • Clean criminal record because the business works with children
  • Photography experience is welcome but not required

Frequently asked questions

What candidates usually want to know.

Do I need professional photography experience?

No. SRP teaches photography skills to entrepreneurs and business skills to photographers. A willingness to learn and work with children matters most.

How much capital should I have access to?

The current franchise website says candidates should possess or have access to approximately $50,000–$60,000. Review the current FDD for complete fees, costs, definitions, and financing considerations.

Is the business home-based?

Yes. Owners run home-based businesses and take mobile studios to preschools, private schools, K-12 schools, and related events.

How quickly can an owner launch?

The current pre-launch program typically spans about 60–90 days, with a goal of photographing the first school within roughly two months after training. Timing varies.

What ongoing support is included?

Support includes weekly growth meetings, monthly system meetings, one-on-one calls, an annual conference, peer networking, emergency assistance, ongoing training tools, and new seasonal set designs.

Is there a veteran incentive?

SRP's current veterans program describes qualified-veteran discounts on the franchise fee plus personalized onboarding and support. Confirm current terms in the FDD and directly with the franchise team.
