Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers — no sales pressure.

Are you cheaper than the dealer?

Yes — typically 30-50% less on labor and parts for the same OEM-quality work. We use the same factory diagnostic tools and OE or premium-equivalent parts.

Will independent service void my warranty?

No. Federal Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act protects you. We use OE-spec parts and document everything to manufacturer standards so your warranty stays intact.

Do you work on cars under extended warranty?

Yes. We work with most major extended-warranty companies and can handle claims paperwork on your behalf.

Can you do a pre-purchase inspection on a used European car?

Absolutely — and we strongly recommend it. Our 100-point PPI on used BMW, Mercedes, Audi, Porsche, and Volvo regularly catches $5K-$15K issues before you sign anything.

How long has Mastermind been in Denver?

Since 2002. 24 years of repeat customers, four-generation families bringing every European car they've owned. Reputation is the whole business.

Do you offer financing?

Yes — we partner with Sunbit and several auto-repair financing programs so larger jobs (timing belts, transmissions, suspension overhauls) don't have to wait.
