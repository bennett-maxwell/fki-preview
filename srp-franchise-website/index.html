Why SRP

Six Reasons Franchisees Choose Spoiled Rotten Photography

Every benefit below is built into the model — not a promise, a pattern.

Repeat Customers

Families return year after year for school photography, building a loyal, recurring customer base session after session.

High Ticket

The average parent spends $96 per portrait package, giving franchisees strong revenue per session from day one.

High Volume

With roughly 28 kids per school session and $1,900+ per photographer per day, volume adds up fast — average franchisee sales reached $279,000+ in 2024, with the top third averaging $558,000+.

Highly Profitable

System-wide sales are up 80% since 2022, customer orders up 81%, and existing franchise sales up 28% — a model built for margin.

Flexible Schedule

Spring and fall are busy school-photography seasons; the rest of the year opens up for marketing, planning, and time off.

Fun to Operate

Working with kids to capture unforgettable childhood memories makes this a business owners genuinely enjoy running.

FAQ

Questions Franchise Candidates Ask

What are the start-up costs to open a Spoiled Rotten Photography franchise?

The initial franchise fee is $39,000, with total initial investment ranging from $52,695 to $64,350. See FDD Item 19 for details.

What are average franchisee sales?

Average franchisee sales in 2024 were $279,000+, with the top third of franchisees averaging $558,000+. See FDD Item 19 for details.

Who is the ideal candidate?

People who enjoy working with kids and want a flexible schedule that follows the school year, paired with a highly profitable, low-cost mobile business that fits in any SUV or small pickup.

Is financing available?

Franchise ownership requires meeting net worth and liquid capital requirements reviewed during qualification; financing specifics are discussed directly with the franchise team.

Why do customers choose Spoiled Rotten Photography?

SRP is ranked the #1 Child Services Franchise and delivers unforgettable school photography experiences that capture childhood memories, with an average parent spend of $96 per portrait package.

How do SRP franchisees compete in the market?

SRP franchisees operate inside an $11B portrait photography market (2022) growing 4-5% per year through 2030, while SRP franchisees themselves are growing sales 10%+ per year.

What training and support is provided?

New franchisees complete a 60-90 day pre-launch program of self-paced online modules and video conferences over about a month, followed by in-person training at corporate headquarters.

Do franchisees get a protected territory?

Yes, Spoiled Rotten Photography franchisees receive a protected territory.

Is this a home-based business?

Yes. The mobile studio fits in any SUV or small pickup, making it one of the lowest-cost franchise models to launch and operate.
