Reading this lot’s report

On lot DPS-6576225 the laboratory reports BPC-157 at 99.876% purity (9.97 mg measured), TB-500 at 99.66% (9.93 mg) and GHK-Cu at 99.82% (49.88 mg). Read each component against its own labeled amount, not against the 70 mg total.

What the public report does not show

The COA library lists this report as UPLC/MS covering purity, identity and quantitation. No separate endotoxin, sterility, elemental-impurity or residual-solvent report is published for lot DPS-6576225 as of 2026-10-10. Renvara states a seven-point program; this page does not claim that this lot passed all seven. See what each test measures, or how to read a COA.
