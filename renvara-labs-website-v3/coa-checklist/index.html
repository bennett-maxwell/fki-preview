The 60-second COA checklist

Tick ten boxes: product, strength and lot match; the analyzed date, laboratory and report number are shown; the method is listed; every component has a result; the measured quantity sits beside the label; the scope is clear; and you know who to ask. If any box stays empty, do not assume the report applies to your vial.

Renvara Labs. For laboratory research use only. Match a report to one lot.

  1. Product name matches
    The name on the report is the name on your vial. If a catalog code is used, confirm it maps to the compound.
  2. Labeled strength matches
    The strength on the report is the strength on your label, for example 10 mg.
  3. Lot number matches exactly
    Compare character by character. A report for a different lot does not describe your vial.
  4. Analyzed date is shown
    The date the laboratory tested the sample is printed on the report.
  5. Laboratory and report number are shown
    The PDF opens on the laboratory’s own site, not only on the seller’s.
  6. Method is listed
    For Renvara’s six public reports this is UPLC/MS purity, identity and quantitation.
  7. Every component has a result
    A blend shows a purity and a quantity for each peptide, not one combined figure.
  8. Measured quantity sits beside the label
    Compare the measured quantity with the labeled strength.
  9. Scope is clear
    A purity report is not an endotoxin, sterility, metals or solvents report. Note what is not listed.
  10. Know who to ask
    If anything does not match, email support@renvaralabs.com with the product, lot and order number.
