What our public COAs show today, and what is not published yet

The public reports show purity, identity and quantitation by UPLC/MS for one lot of each of six products. They do not show endotoxin, sterility, elemental-impurity or residual-solvent results. Renvara states a seven-point program performed in Utah; this site does not say that any lot passed all seven tests.

Shown today

  • Product name and labeled strength
  • Lot number and analyzed date (2026-09-16 on all six)
  • Laboratory name and report number (Horizon Analytical)
  • Method listed as UPLC/MS
  • Purity percent and measured quantity for each component

Not published yet

  • Endotoxin (LAL/BET), sterility, elemental-impurity and residual-solvent results

What is a certificate of analysis?

A certificate of analysis documents laboratory test results for a specific sample or batch. Check the report for the product identity, lot number, testing methods, results and laboratory details.

Which report applies to my vial?

Use the certificate with the same product, strength and lot number as your vial. A report for a different batch does not describe the batch you received.

What if I cannot find my lot number?

Email support@renvaralabs.com with the product name, the lot number and, if available, your order number.
