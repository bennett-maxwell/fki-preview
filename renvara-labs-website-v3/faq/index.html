Certificates of analysis

What is a certificate of analysis (COA)?

A COA documents laboratory test results for a specific sample or lot. Check the report for the product identity, lot number, methods, results and laboratory details. It describes the lot named on the report, not every lot of a product.

Which report applies to my vial?

Use the report with the same product, strength and lot number as your vial. A report for a different lot does not describe the lot you received. The COA library lists six public lot reports as of 2026-10-10.

What do the public COAs cover today?

The six public reports in the COA library are listed as UPLC/MS purity, identity and quantitation, all analyzed on 2026-09-16 by Horizon Analytical. They are not endotoxin, sterility, elemental-impurity or residual-solvent reports.

Does every lot pass all seven tests?

Renvara states that its seven-point program is performed in Utah. The public COAs do not show all seven results for any lot, so this site does not claim that a lot passed all seven. Ask support for the documentation that exists for your lot.

What if I cannot find my lot number?

Email support@renvaralabs.com with the product name, the lot number and, if you have it, your order number. Support can help locate the relevant documentation.

Ordering and eligibility

Who can order?

Adults aged 21 or older who are qualified researchers or authorized laboratory purchasers and who will use products only for lawful in vitro or laboratory research. Personal or household experimentation does not qualify.

Do I need an account to see prices?

Yes. The product catalog, prices and stock are visible after sign-in at renvaralabs.com. This preview shows specifications and lot reports without an account and marks pricing as 'Sign in to see pricing'.

What does registration ask for?

Name, email, mobile phone, researcher type, an eligibility attestation and agreement to the terms. A credential upload (PDF, JPG or PNG up to 5 MB) is optional. Renvara may ask for more documentation before accepting an order.

Shipping and discounts

How much is shipping?

For U.S. orders: DHL Ground is $12.99 under $150 and free at $150 and above. FedEx 2Day is $19.99 under $150, $6.99 from $150 to $199.99, and free at $200 and above. Next Day is $32.99 at every order value.

How are shipping thresholds calculated?

On the merchandise subtotal after volume discounts and coupon codes, before tax and shipping. Delivery times are estimates after shipment: 3 to 8 days for DHL Ground, 2 days for FedEx 2Day, 1 day for Next Day.

What discounts are available?

WELCOME10 takes 10% off a first order, once per customer, with no minimum. Volume discounts apply automatically within the same product and vial strength: 5% off 2 to 4 vials, 10% off 5 to 9 vials, 15% off 10 or more.

Can discounts be combined?

Yes. WELCOME10 applies after the volume discount, so the combined maximum is 23.5%. The two discounts apply in sequence and do not add up to 25%.

Returns and research use

Can I return an order?

No. Renvara does not accept returns after shipment. Report visible damage, missing items or incorrect products within three calendar days of delivery, and do not send materials back without authorization.

Can an order be cancelled?

The Shipping and Returns policy states that orders cannot be cancelled after checkout, because orders are dispatched promptly. This preview shows that stricter published rule.

What does research use only mean?

Products are supplied for laboratory research only. They are not for human or veterinary use, diagnostic use or use in food, and they have not been evaluated by the FDA. Testing describes the sample tested and does not make a product suitable for any other use.
