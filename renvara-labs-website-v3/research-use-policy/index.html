Research use policy

Who may order, what the products may be used for, and what a certificate of analysis does and does not establish.

Shop the catalog (opens in a new tab)Sign in to see pricing (opens in a new tab)

Who may order

Renvara Labs products are for laboratory research use only. Buyers must be at least 21 years old, meet the research eligibility requirements, and have appropriate training and access to suitable facilities. Products are for lawful in vitro or analytical laboratory research. They are not for human or veterinary use, and personal or household experimentation does not qualify.

Eligibility in practice

  • You must be at least 21 years old and able to enter the agreement.
  • Independent researchers must operate in a controlled laboratory setting.
  • Registration asks for name, email, mobile phone, researcher type and an eligibility attestation. A credential upload is optional.
  • Renvara may ask for documentation of identity, laboratory affiliation or intended research before accepting an order, and may decline a purchase when eligibility cannot be established.

What the products may and may not be used for

Show the list: What the products may and may not be used for

Permitted

  • Lawful in vitro research
  • Analytical laboratory research
  • Research by commercial organizations that stays within these limits

Not permitted

  • Any use in humans or animals, by any route, including personal experimentation
  • Clinical, diagnostic or therapeutic use
  • Food, consumer or household products, or compounding

Resale, redistribution or repackaging requires prior written authorization from Renvara and must keep all research restrictions with the material. A product name, an account, an accepted order or a certificate of analysis does not authorize a prohibited use or show regulatory approval. Renvara does not provide medical advice.

What a certificate does and does not establish

A certificate of analysis describes the sample, methods and results named in that report. Do not assume that a report for one lot applies to another, or that a purity result establishes sterility, endotoxin status, clinical safety or fitness for a particular experiment. Only documented testing supports the characteristics a report states, and testing does not authorize human or animal use.
