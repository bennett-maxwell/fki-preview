Discounts at a glance

WELCOME10 takes 10% off a first order, once per customer with no minimum. Volume discounts of 5%, 10% and 15% apply automatically to 2 to 4, 5 to 9 and 10 or more vials of the same product and strength. They apply in sequence for a combined maximum of 23.5%, and U.S. DHL Ground shipping is free from $150.

U.S. shipping options and thresholds

Shipping thresholds use your merchandise subtotal after volume discounts and coupon codes, before tax and shipping. DHL Ground is $12.99 under $150 and free from $150. FedEx 2Day is $19.99 under $150, $6.99 from $150 to $199.99 and free from $200. Next Day is $32.99 at every order value.

Returns, damage and cancellations

Renvara does not accept returns after shipment, because research materials need controlled handling and traceability. Report visible damage, missing items or incorrect products within three calendar days of delivery, with the order number, lot information and photographs. Do not send materials back without authorization. Confirmed errors are addressed by replacement or refund.
