🧠 How the AI thinks (plain words)

Sub-agents — small helpers splitting up the work

When you give the AI a big task, it sends out smaller helper AIs to work on different parts at the same time — like sending 4 people to 4 different counters at the DMV instead of waiting in one line.

Say: "Fan this out — run each part at the same time."

Self-audit loops — the helper checks itself before saying done

Instead of just finishing and saying "done," the AI can run a check on its own work before handing it to you. Like a surgeon who checks their own work before closing up. This is what the overnight sweep does.

Say: "Self-check this before you call it done."

Green / Amber / Red decision tiers

Not every decision should be made by the AI alone. Green = AI handles it alone. Amber = AI gives you a draft, you approve. Red = you handle it yourself, AI just supports. Our entire sales playbook is now sorted into these three tiers.

Say: "Is this a green, amber, or red decision for us?"
