Your AI System, Applied to Your Store

Overview of the AI Employees

Your Blueprint defines six AI Employees for Indy Clover's franchise development operation. At the store level, each one does the equivalent job for booth-rental operations — the same underlying employee, pointed at your store's seller and buyer workflows instead of the franchise-candidate pipeline.

Franchise-Candidate Speed-to-Lead Employee
Blueprint role: answers every franchise-candidate inquiry in under 60 seconds, 24/7, qualifies liquid capital and eligibility.
Store application: the same speed-to-lead logic applied to every inbound seller and buyer inquiry about your booths — a fast, on-brand first response the moment someone asks about renting a booth or visiting your store, day or night.
Discovery-Day Prep & Follow-Up Employee
Blueprint role: builds a candidate brief before every Discovery Day and drafts the same-day follow-up.
Store application: builds a seller-prospect brief before a booth walkthrough or tour — what they asked about, what they sell, what booth size fits — and drafts the same-day follow-up so no interested seller goes quiet.
Franchisee Onboarding Employee
Blueprint role: tracks the ramp from signing to Grand Opening — site selection, construction, training, loan processing.
Store application: tracks a new seller's ramp from first inquiry to booth set up — agreement signed, booth assigned, Saturday setup completed, first listings live — so no step falls through the cracks.
Franchisee Support & Content Employee
Blueprint role: turns real franchisee and seller stories into a week of candidate-facing content in Indy Clover's brand voice.
Store application: turns your store's real booth highlights, Discount Day promos, and community events into a week of buyer-facing social and email content — the exact same content engine, aimed at shoppers instead of franchise candidates.
Pipeline Intelligence Employee
Blueprint role: reads the candidate pipeline daily, flags stalls, drafts re-engagement for approval.
Store application: reads your seller waitlist and open-booth inquiries daily, flags any seller conversation that's gone quiet, and drafts the re-engagement touch for your approval — so an interested seller never gets lost.
Franchise Development Reporting Employee
Blueprint role: delivers a leadership snapshot — candidate pipeline, Discovery Days booked, royalty status, location count.
Store application: delivers a scheduled snapshot for your store — booths occupied vs. capacity, seller waitlist size, upcoming booth renewals, and buyer traffic trend — so you run the store from current numbers, not a memory of last week.

Getting Set Up

Roles & Access Needed

What your store needs to have ready before kickoff so the rollout stays on the 3-day / 7-day timeline.

From You (the Franchisee)

  • Owner or store manager sign-off on the approval gate for external sends
  • Access to your store's inquiry channels (phone line, DM accounts, email, walk-in intake if used)
  • Access to your team messaging tool so staff can see AI Employee outputs
  • Current booth roster: occupied vs. open, capacity, upcoming renewals
  • Any existing seller agreement templates or onboarding materials

From FKI

  • AI Employee build, brand-voice calibration, and connection to your store's tools
  • Human-approval gate configured for every external send by default
  • 30-day performance review and refinement based on your store's real data
  • Ongoing AI operations support after Day 30

Before You Go Live

Calibration Checklist

Confirm each of these before your first AI Employee goes live, so Day 1 output already sounds like your store.

Brand voice confirmed — friendly, playful, upbeat, inclusive, clear, authentic, per Indy Clover's documented voice guide; no "thrift store" or "bargain-bin" language.
Approval gate set — every AI-drafted external send (to sellers or buyers) routes to a named human before it goes out.
State-block and compliance rules loaded — any earnings reference carries the FDD Item 19 qualifier; no guaranteed-outcome language.
Booth roster and capacity current — occupied vs. open booths, pricing, and Discount Days schedule (Thursday & Friday) reflected accurately.
Seller onboarding sequence reviewed — welcome message, Saturday setup instructions, first-week check-in match your store's actual process.
Content sourcing agreed — who submits real booth stories and community events for the Content Employee to turn into posts.
Live test completed — a test seller inquiry sent and the fast response verified before go-live.
