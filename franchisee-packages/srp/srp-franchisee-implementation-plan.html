Blueprint AI Implementation Plan — SRP Franchisees

The same 3-day / 7-day / 30-day standard Franchise KI Consulting runs for every SRP territory, translated into the exact actions you take as the local operator — school outreach, picture-day prep, and parent communication.

Your AI System

The 6 AI Employees Behind This Plan

Each AI Employee maps to a specific part of your territory's workflow — school accounts, franchisee support, seasonal content, parent ordering, onboarding, and admin reporting. This plan walks through how each one comes online in your territory.

1
Franchise-Candidate Speed-to-Lead Employee
Answers every inbound franchise-candidate inquiry about SRP's home-based, school-partnership model quickly, qualifies liquid capital and background fit, and routes the candidate to the right pipeline stage.
2
School-Account & Franchisee Support Employee
Tracks each school partnership's renewal timeline, drafts the outreach that protects your renewal rate, and fields routine franchisee questions so onboarding goes faster.
3
Content & Seasonal Campaign Employee
Turns each picture-day cycle into parent ordering reminders, school-outreach materials, and franchise-candidate stories — in SRP's warm, family-first voice.
4
Parent Ordering & Fulfillment Employee
Keeps parents informed from picture day through portrait delivery — order confirmations, ordering-window reminders, and delivery updates.
5
Franchisee Onboarding Employee
Walks new operators through training milestones ahead of their first picture day, using SRP's documented training materials.
6
Franchisor Admin & Reporting Employee
Pulls school-account status, renewal health, and franchisee activity into a scheduled snapshot for the franchisor's system-wide view.

Milestones

Days 1-3 through Month 2+

No long multi-month rollouts. Here is exactly what happens in your territory at each milestone, expanded into concrete franchisee actions.

Days 1-3First AI Employee Live
Result: Every candidate inquiry gets a fast, on-brand response — and your school-outreach calendar is mapped.
What FKI Sets Up
  • Kickoff call mapping your candidate pipeline, school-account calendar, and approval gates
  • Franchise-Candidate Speed-to-Lead Employee connected to your inbound channels
  • Brand voice built from SRP's documented brand guide — warm, family-focused, culture-first — before launch, not after
  • Live test: submit a test inquiry and watch the response fire
Franchisee Actions
  • School outreach cadence: confirm your current list of school contacts (preschools, daycares, childcare centers) so the AI Employee has accurate account data from day one
  • Parent communication flows: share any parent-facing templates you already use, so early content matches your existing voice
  • Access setup: connect your CRM/pipeline tool and inbound email so inquiries route correctly
Days 4-7All 6 Employees Running
Result: All 6 AI Employees deployed in real workflows. Team trained and using the system.
What FKI Sets Up
  • School-Account & Franchisee Support Employee tracking renewal windows
  • Content & Seasonal Campaign Employee producing picture-day content
  • Parent Ordering & Fulfillment Employee live on ordering communications
  • Franchisee Onboarding Employee supporting new operators
  • Franchisor Admin & Reporting Employee on its reporting schedule
  • Team walkthrough: no technical knowledge required
Franchisee Actions
  • Picture-day season prep: load your upcoming picture-day dates so the Content & Seasonal Campaign Employee can build parent reminders and school materials around real dates
  • School outreach cadence: review the renewal-window flags for your active school accounts and confirm which relationships need a personal call versus automated outreach
  • Parent communication flows: approve the ordering-window reminder and delivery-update templates before they go live to families
  • Walk through the system with your team so everyone knows what's automated and what still needs a human sign-off
Day 30Calibrated to SRP Data
Result: System knows SRP's business. Every employee calibrated on real operating data.
What FKI Sets Up
  • 30-day performance review: response rates, renewal-outreach hits, hours recovered
  • Employees refined on your actual school-account and candidate patterns
  • Expansion roadmap: what to hand over next as your territory grows
Franchisee Actions
  • School outreach cadence: compare the renewal outreach the AI Employee drafted against what actually kept schools engaged, and flag any tone or timing adjustments
  • Picture-day season prep: review how the seasonal content performed around your most recent picture day and note what to reuse next cycle
  • Confirm which routine franchisee-support questions the AI Employee handled well, and which still need franchisor input
Month 2+Compounding
Result: AI runs like a trained team member who never forgets and never has a bad season.
What FKI Sets Up
  • Minimal manual input required from you or your team

  • You own the system — FKI stays as the AI operations partner
Franchisee Actions
  • School outreach cadence: shift your personal time toward new school prospecting, since renewal follow-through is running on schedule
