## What to Skip (For Now)

Sequencing, Not Limits 

### What SRP Deliberately Saves for Later — So the Big Wins Land First

Here is the honest truth: AI has reached the point where almost anything a person does on a computer can eventually be handled by a trained AI Employee — given the right access and the right training. The plan is to hand that work over step by step, highest-impact first. These are the items FKI is holding for later phases of SRP’s system. Not because AI cannot get there — because chasing them on day one would distract from the employees that move the needle first. 

 › 

Autonomous External Sending 
AI Employees draft every school- and parent-facing message — but a human approves every external send during SRP’s pre-launch phase. Absolutely automatable later; for now the approval gate is a feature, not a limitation. 

 › 

Autonomous Territory & Pricing Decisions 
AI can model territory density and school-market data — and eventually help prep the analysis further. For now, territory decisions and investment terms stay with the franchisor. A later-phase win, not a today distraction. 

 › 

FDD & Legal Review 
AI drafts FDD-adjacent positioning observations from documented brand facts, but franchise sales is a regulated category — qualified counsel reviews anything with legal weight, especially while SRP’s FDD audit is in progress. AI will take more of the prep work over time; the sign-off stays human. 

 › 

Fully Autonomous Candidate & School Conversations 
AI preps the brief, drafts the follow-up, and tracks the renewal calendar — but a candidate deciding on a ~$60K investment (individual results vary; see Item 19 of the applicable FDD for financial performance representations), or a school director deciding on a multi-year contract, deserves a real person in the room. AI makes sure that conversation is never wasted on admin.
