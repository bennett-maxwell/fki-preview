The System

The 6 AI Employees Built for Your Unit

Each employee maps to a specific workflow inside a real Hello Sugar suite or flagship operation — new-client booking, rebooking on the 4–6 week cycle, membership growth, reputation, front-desk overflow, and owner reporting.

1
Speed-to-Lead Booking Employee
Watches every inbound channel for new-client inquiries, drafts a warm and fast first response, confirms service interest, and gets the client onto your automated booking platform's calendar before they book with another salon.
2
Rebooking & Recall Employee
Tracks each client's natural 4–6 week waxing and sugaring service cycle and drafts the rebooking outreach at the right moment — the single highest-leverage employee in a recurring-service beauty business.
3
Membership Growth Employee
Identifies clients on their second or third visit — the natural window for a membership conversion conversation — and drafts a clear, no-pressure membership offer tied to the recurring value of the 4–6 week cycle.
4
Review & Reputation Employee
Sends a well-timed review request after a positive visit signal and drafts thoughtful responses to incoming Google reviews in your unit's voice, flagging any negative review for a personal follow-up.
5
Front-Desk Overflow / FAQ Employee
Answers routine client questions — hours, service prep, cancellation policy, membership terms — using only your unit's documented information, so the floor manager and estheticians stay focused on the floor.
6
Owner Dashboard & Reporting Employee
Pulls booking volume, rebooking rate, membership conversions, and review activity into a scheduled snapshot, so a semi-absentee owner sees exactly how the unit is performing without logging into the booking platform daily.

Before Kickoff

Roles & Access Needed

Nothing exotic — the systems your unit already runs. Here is what FKI needs access to before Days 1–3 can start.

Automated Booking Platform
Read access to scheduling and client records so the Speed-to-Lead and Rebooking & Recall Employees can see availability and service history.
CRM / SMS / Email
Access to the channel your unit uses for client communication — needed for rebooking outreach, membership offers, and front-desk overflow replies.
Google Business Profile / Reviews
Access to your review profile so the Review & Reputation Employee can send requests and draft responses in your unit's voice.
Service & Membership Menu (Documented)
Current, documented pricing and membership terms — the compliance boundary every employee is built around. No employee quotes undocumented pricing.
Approval Gate Owner
Who signs off on external sends at launch — typically the owner or floor manager — until the human approval gate is lifted in a later phase.
Finance & Reporting Access
Whatever your unit already uses for booking volume and revenue tracking, so the Owner Dashboard & Reporting Employee has a real source to summarize.

Before Go-Live

Calibration Checklist

Membership tiers and terms documentedNeeded before the Membership Growth Employee can present accurate, no-pressure offers.
