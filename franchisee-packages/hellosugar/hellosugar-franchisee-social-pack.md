### LinkedIn Post 3
**Hook:** A waxing and sugaring unit runs on a 4-6 week clock. Most systems don't track it.

**Body:**
Waxing and sugaring services carry a natural, recurring 4-6 week rebooking cycle — that's inherent to hair growth patterns, not a marketing construct. It's also the single highest-leverage recurring-revenue lever a Hello Sugar unit has.

Most booking systems can send a generic reminder. Few track each client's actual cycle and time the outreach to the moment they're likely to rebook — not too early, not so late they've drifted to another salon.

That's the job of a dedicated Rebooking & Recall Employee inside the Blueprint AI system: track the cycle per client, draft the outreach, protect the chairs staying full.
