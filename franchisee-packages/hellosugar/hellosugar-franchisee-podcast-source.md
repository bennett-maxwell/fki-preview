## Section 8 — What You're Deliberately Saving for Later

Here's an honest truth: AI has reached the point where almost anything a person does on a computer could eventually be handled by a trained AI Employee, given the right access and training. The plan is to hand that work over step by step, highest-impact first. A few things we're intentionally holding back — not because AI can't get there, but because chasing them on day one would distract from what moves the needle first.

Autonomous external sending: AI Employees draft every client-facing message, but a human approves every external send at launch. That's a feature right now, not a limitation.

Autonomous pricing and membership-tier decisions: AI can surface which clients are ready for a membership offer and draft the outreach, but pricing and tier structure stay with you and franchisor guidance.

FDD and legal review: AI drafts FDD-adjacent positioning from documented brand facts, but franchise matters with legal weight go through qualified counsel — always.

Fully autonomous client conversations: AI preps the brief, drafts the follow-up, and tracks the rebooking calendar, but sensitive conversations, complaints, and judgment calls about guest experience deserve a real person. AI's job is making sure that conversation is never wasted on admin.

## Section 9 — The 6 AI Employees Built for Your Unit

Let's walk through all six, one at a time.

**Employee One: Speed-to-Lead Booking Employee.** This watches every inbound channel for new-client inquiries about your suite or flagship location. It drafts a warm, fast first response, confirms the service they're interested in, and gets them onto your booking platform's calendar before they book somewhere else. It never quotes pricing without confirming it against your current service menu. Outcome: fast response to every booking inquiry, no chair-time opportunity goes cold.

**Employee Two: Rebooking and Recall Employee.** This tracks each client's natural 4–6 week waxing and sugaring cycle and drafts the rebooking outreach at the right moment — not too early, not so late they've drifted elsewhere. This is arguably the single highest-leverage employee in your entire system, because it protects the recurring-revenue engine your business is actually built on. Outcome: rebooking windows tracked automatically, chairs stay full without a front-desk hire.

**Employee Three: Membership Growth Employee.** This identifies clients on their second or third visit — the natural window for a membership conversation — and drafts a clear, no-pressure offer tied to the recurring value of the 4–6 week cycle. It never overstates savings and never pressures. Outcome: consistent membership offers at the right moment, recurring revenue instead of one-off visits.

**Employee Four: Review and Reputation Employee.** This sends a well-timed review request after a positive visit signal and drafts thoughtful responses to incoming Google reviews in your voice. Any negative review gets flagged for you or your floor manager to handle personally — never an automated reply to a complaint. Outcome: consistent review flow, protecting your local search visibility.

**Employee Five: Front-Desk Overflow and FAQ Employee.** This answers routine client questions — hours, service prep, cancellation policy, membership terms — using only your unit's documented information, so your floor manager and estheticians aren't pulled off the floor for repetitive questions. Outcome: routine questions answered instantly, the floor stays focused on clients in the chair.

**Employee Six: Owner Dashboard and Reporting Employee.** This pulls booking volume, rebooking rate, membership conversions, and review activity into a scheduled snapshot, so you see exactly how your unit is performing without logging into the booking platform daily. This is the employee that protects your 10-hours-a-week model directly. Outcome: a scheduled, plain-English snapshot — no more manual checking.
