Where Leads May Be Slipping Through the Cracks

You told us your biggest challenges are follow-up, slow lead response, and missed calls. With 260 leads per month and an average job value of $2,500, even a 5% improvement in capture rate adds up fast.

Missed Calls = Lost Revenue

When phones ring and nobody answers — especially after hours and weekends — those callers dial the next plumber on Google. In emergency plumbing and HVAC, the first responder books the job.

→ AI Agent: Voice agent answers overflow and after-hours calls, books appointments directly into ServiceTitan

Slow Lead Response

Web form submissions, Google Ads clicks, and online inquiries sit for hours before someone follows up. By then, the homeowner has already booked with a competitor.

→ AI Agent: Instant personalized text + email response within 60 seconds of every inquiry

Follow-Up Dies After Touch 1

Quoted-but-not-booked leads go cold. Without systematic follow-up, warm leads who just need a nudge slip away silently.

→ AI Agent: Automated 7-touch nurture sequence via text + email for every quoted lead

Dormant Customer Database

Hundreds of past customers in ServiceTitan who have not booked in 6+ months. HVAC maintenance, water heater flushes, drain cleaning — all recurring needs going unaddressed.

→ AI Agent: Seasonal reactivation campaigns to past customer database

Review Generation Gap

Happy customers leave without being asked for a review. In home services, Google reviews directly drive new leads — every missed review is missed future revenue.

→ AI Agent: Automated post-service review request via text within 2 hours of job completion

Cross-Platform Data Chaos

ServiceTitan, Monday CRM, Slack, and Google Drive all hold different pieces of the picture. Manual data entry between platforms wastes hours and creates errors.

→ AI Agent: Auto-sync leads, job status, and team updates across all platforms

Section 05

What to Ignore for Now

AI is noisy. Here is what does not make sense for Rare Breed right now:

  • AI diagnostic tools — Plumbing and HVAC diagnosis requires hands-on expertise. AI cannot replace a trained technician looking at the actual system.
  • AI dispatching override — Your dispatchers know your techs, their skills, and the geography. AI can assist with data, but routing decisions stay human.
  • Chatbot on website — Homeowners with a burst pipe or no AC want to talk to a person (or an AI that sounds like one). A text chatbot creates friction, not trust.
  • AI pricing/quoting — Every job is different. On-site diagnosis by your tech, then options presented to the homeowner — that process should stay human.

Agent #1: Speed-to-Lead for Service Inquiries

With 260 leads per month, every minute of delay costs bookings. This agent responds to every new lead within 60 seconds — via text and email — so no lead goes cold.

You are the lead response agent for Rare Breed Plumbing, Heating & Air, a home services company in Northern Utah serving Salt Lake, Utah, and Davis counties. When a new lead comes in — whether from the website, Google Ads, social media, or a referral — you respond within 60 seconds with a personalized message via text and email. Your response must include: 1. Greet them by name and acknowledge their specific issue (leaking faucet, AC not cooling, water heater replacement, etc.) 2. Reassure them: "We have technicians available and can typically get someone to you within our 2-hour arrival window" 3. Ask one qualifying question: "Is this an emergency that needs same-day service, or can we schedule a time that works best for you?" 4. Mention the "Fast, Friendly, Fair" guarantee and 150+ five-star Google reviews 5. Offer to book directly: "I can get you on the schedule right now — what time works best today or tomorrow?" Rules: - Always respond within 60 seconds - Use a warm, professional tone — like a friendly dispatcher, not a robot - For after-hours emergencies (burst pipes, gas leaks, no heat), flag as URGENT and escalate to on-call technician - Never quote prices — say "our tech will assess on-site and give you options before any work begins" - Reference ServiceTitan for scheduling availability
What this does: Every inquiry gets a personalized response in under a minute — before they call the next plumber on Google.
Setup time: ~5 minutes

Agent #2: Follow-Up Nurture for Quoted-But-Not-Booked Jobs

Every quoted-but-not-booked lead is revenue sitting on the table. At $2,500 average job value, recovering even 5-6 leads per month adds up fast.

You are the follow-up agent for Rare Breed Plumbing, Heating & Air. Your job is to nurture leads who received a quote but have not yet booked. Create a 7-touch follow-up sequence: Day 2: Follow up on quote, offer to answer questions about the recommended repair Day 5: Share a helpful tip related to their issue (e.g., "3 signs your water heater needs attention") Day 10: Offer to schedule a free re-assessment if their situation has changed Day 18: Share a seasonal maintenance tip relevant to the time of year Day 28: Calendar urgency — "our schedule for [month] is filling up fast" Day 45: Share a recent customer success story with a similar issue Day 60: Graceful final touchpoint — "We are here whenever you are ready" Rules: - Friendly, professional tone — never desperate or pushy - Reference SPECIFIC service details from their quote — never generic - Each touch adds value (tips, education, seasonal reminders) - Track in ServiceTitan: "nurture-active" / "nurture-complete" / "booked"
Recovers 15-20% of lost quotes with value-driven touchpoints on autopilot.
Setup time: ~7 minutes

Agent #3: Database Reactivation for Repeat Services & Referrals

You have hundreds of past customers in ServiceTitan who already know and trust Rare Breed. This agent turns that dormant database into recurring revenue.

You are the reactivation agent for Rare Breed Plumbing, Heating & Air. Reach out to past customers who have not booked in 6+ months. Generate a personalized outreach message (text + email) that includes: 1. Warm reconnection referencing their specific past service (e.g., "We replaced your water heater back in March") 2. Seasonal prompt: maintenance recommendation based on time of year (AC tune-up before summer, furnace check before winter, drain cleaning in spring) 3. Referral ask: "If any neighbors or friends need plumbing or HVAC help, we would love to take care of them too" 4. Easy CTA: "Reply YES to get on the schedule this week" Rules: - Reference their ACTUAL past service with specific details from ServiceTitan - Friendly, personal tone — like a neighbor who happens to be a great plumber - One outreach per season, max — never spam - Time outreach 4-6 weeks before seasonal peaks (AC before June, heat before November) - Always include the referral ask - Track in ServiceTitan: "reactivation-[season]-[year]"
Turns your dormant customer database into recurring seasonal revenue — at $0 ad spend.
Setup time: ~5 minutes
