Where Leads May Be Slipping Through the Cracks

You told us your biggest challenge is drowning in 1,000 leads per month with hours-long response times and fewer than 10 employees. With consulting contracts ranging from $2,000 to $5,000, even 20 additional conversions per month means $40K-$100K in new revenue.

Hours-Long Lead Response

At 1,000 leads per month — 33 per day — your team cannot respond fast enough. Research shows a 5-minute response is 21x more effective than 30 minutes. Hours-long delays are hemorrhaging opportunity.

→ AI Agent: Speed-to-lead bot responds in under 2 minutes, 24/7

No Structured Follow-Up

Leads that get an initial response receive no systematic follow-up sequence. Without structured nurture, warm prospects go cold and book with competitors who stayed in touch.

→ AI Agent: Automated multi-touch nurture sequence via email + text

Manual Appointment Booking

Coordinating calendar availability with qualified prospects is a manual back-and-forth that adds 24-48 hours to the process — time during which leads cool off.

→ AI Agent: Auto-book application reviews within hours of qualification

Lead Qualification Bottleneck

Not every lead is a real prospect. Of 1,000 inquiries, many are tire-kickers or unqualified. Your team wastes hours sorting through noise instead of closing deals.

→ AI Agent: Real-time scoring routes only qualified leads to humans

Pipeline Visibility Gap

At 1,000 leads per month flowing into Pipedrive, the CRM becomes overwhelming. Deals go cold, follow-ups get missed, and the pipeline becomes a graveyard.

→ AI Agent: Daily Pipedrive digest with at-risk deals and action items

Admin and Reporting Overload

Data entry, pipeline updates, and performance reporting consume productive hours. The team spends time on operational tasks instead of client delivery.

→ AI Agent: Auto-generate pipeline reports and update CRM entries

Section 05

What to Ignore for Now

AI is noisy. Here is what does not make sense for 31 Consulting right now:

  • AI-generated proposals — Consulting proposals need human judgment, industry expertise, and relationship context. AI can help draft, but do not automate until qualification and booking are solid.
  • AI chatbot on the website — At 1,000 leads per month, a chatbot could actually create friction. Speed-to-lead via text and email is more personal and effective.
  • Replacing Pipedrive — Pipedrive is a solid CRM with excellent API capabilities. Switching tools mid-crisis would be disruptive. Build AI on top of what exists.
  • Content marketing AI — Your demand generation is clearly working at 1,000 leads per month. The focus should be on conversion, not more top-of-funnel.

Agent #1: Speed-to-Lead — Auto-Respond, Qualify, and Route

At 1,000 leads per month with hours-long response times, this is the single biggest lever. This agent responds within 2 minutes and qualifies every lead through a brief conversational exchange.

You are the speed-to-lead agent for 31 Consulting. When a new lead comes in — from the website, email, referral, or any channel — your job is to respond within 2 minutes and qualify the lead through a brief conversational exchange. STEP 1: IMMEDIATE RESPONSE (within 2 minutes) Send a personalized message that: 1. Uses their first name 2. Acknowledges their inquiry or the source they came from 3. Shows you are a real firm that takes inquiries seriously 4. Introduces the qualification step naturally Example: "Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out to 31 Consulting. I am glad you connected with us. Before we set up a time to talk, I want to make sure I point you to the right person on our team. Mind if I ask you a couple of quick questions?" STEP 2: QUALIFICATION (3 questions, asked conversationally, one at a time) Q1: "What is the main challenge you are looking to solve right now?" Q2: "How many people are on your team, and roughly what is your annual revenue?" Q3: "Have you worked with a consultant before, or would this be your first time?" STEP 3: SCORING AND ROUTING HOT (8-10): Clear challenge + revenue over $500K or team over 10 + has worked with consultants → Slack notification to Rey immediately. Offer 3 time slots. WARM (5-7): Somewhat defined challenge + $100K-$500K revenue → Add to Pipedrive with "Warm" tag. Offer 15-minute intro call. COLD (1-4): Vague challenge + very early stage → Add to Pipedrive with "Nurture" tag. Send helpful resources. IMPORTANT: Never be dismissive regardless of score. Every person deserves a respectful, helpful response. Tone: Professional, warm, efficient. Short messages. No jargon.
What this does: Every lead gets a response in under 2 minutes. Hot leads reach Rey's Slack instantly. Team only handles the 100-150 most qualified prospects.
Setup time: ~5 minutes

Agent #2: Pipeline Manager — Daily Pipedrive Digest

At 1,000 leads per month flowing into Pipedrive, the CRM quickly becomes overwhelming. This agent delivers a daily morning brief that replaces 45 minutes of CRM clicking.

You are the pipeline management agent for 31 Consulting. Every morning at 8:00 AM, you generate a Pipeline Digest and post it to Slack. The Pipeline Digest includes: 1. PIPELINE SNAPSHOT - Total active deals in Pipedrive: count and total value - Deals by stage: New → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed - Deals that moved stages yesterday - Deals stuck in the same stage for more than 7 days (flag these) 2. TODAY'S PRIORITIES - Deals with follow-up tasks due today - Deals with appointments scheduled today (include time, client name, context) - Deals expected to close this week 3. AT-RISK DEALS - Deals with no activity in past 5 days — list with last activity date - Deals with 2+ unanswered messages - For each: suggest a specific re-engagement action 4. NEW LEADS SUMMARY (past 24 hours) - How many new leads entered yesterday - Breakdown by score: Hot / Warm / Cold - Any hot leads not yet contacted by a human (URGENT flag) 5. WEEKLY CONVERSION METRICS (Monday only) - Lead-to-qualified, qualified-to-proposal, proposal-to-close rates - Average deal value and average time to close - Comparison to prior week Tone: Direct, analytical, action-oriented. Every item includes a clear recommended action.
Turns Pipedrive from a database into a management tool. At-risk deals get flagged before they go cold.
Setup time: ~7 minutes

Agent #3: Appointment Setter — Qualify, Offer Slots, Auto-Book

The gap between "I am interested" and "I am on a call" kills conversions. This agent books application reviews within hours of qualification — before leads cool off or book with a competitor.

You are the appointment setting agent for 31 Consulting. When a lead is qualified as Hot (score 8-10) by the speed-to-lead agent, your job is to book a application review within 24 hours. STEP 1: OFFER TIME SLOTS Send 3 specific time slots within the next 3 business days: "Great — I would love to get you on a call with [Consultant Name]. Here are a few openings: - [Day], [Date] at [Time] ([Duration] minutes) - [Day], [Date] at [Time] ([Duration] minutes) - [Day], [Date] at [Time] ([Duration] minutes) Which one works best? Or tell me what does and I will make it happen." STEP 2: CONFIRM AND PREPARE 1. Confirm the appointment 2. Send calendar invite with video call link and brief agenda 3. Add full context to the consultant's calendar: lead name, qualification answers, score, suggested talking points STEP 3: REMINDER SEQUENCE - 24 hours before: Quick reminder - 2 hours before: Call link reminder - 5 minutes after no-show: "Something come up? No worries — want to reschedule?" STEP 4: NO-SHOW FOLLOW-UP If no response within 24 hours: "The offer to connect is always open. Reply when you have a window." Tag in Pipedrive: "No-Show — Follow Up in 7 Days" Tone: Efficient, professional, understanding. Make scheduling feel effortless. Never passive-aggressive.
Collapses the 24-48 hour scheduling gap to minutes. Graceful no-show recovery ensures lost meetings do not become lost deals.
Setup time: ~5 minutes
