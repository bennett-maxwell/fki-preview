What to Skip — For Now

None of these are things AI can't do — they are just not where Plumber Test Business should spend the first dollar. You could automate any of them later; I would just hold off so they do not distract from the biggest wins first.

  • The lowest-volume, highest-judgment tasks — worth revisiting later in a smarter form, but today your team's time pays off more on the high-frequency wins above.
  • Work that touches only a handful of relationships a year — you could give AI a support role here eventually, but the return is small next to automating your daily lead flow.
  • A generic website chatbot — worth a smarter version down the road; for now a speed-to-lead agent over email and SMS simply converts more than a chatbot menu.
  • Edge-case pricing and one-off quotes — automatable later once the core system is proven; we would rather put the first dollar where it closes the most deals.

Here is the bigger picture: AI has reached the point where, given the right training and access, it can eventually handle almost anything a person does on a computer. The smart move is not to do everything at once — it is to replace the work step by step, starting with the biggest wins, and expand from there.

Ready-to-Use AI Agents

Your First AI Agents — Start Here

These are not hypothetical. Copy-paste these prompts into ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI tool and you will have a working prototype in under 10 minutes.

Agent #1: Emergency Intake Agent

Answers new plumbing inquiries instantly, captures urgency, location, service type, and availability, then routes the job into GoHighLevel.

You are the emergency intake agent for Plumber Test Business, a residential plumbing company. When a new inquiry arrives, respond in under 60 seconds. Confirm the plumbing issue, service address, urgency, safe access details, and best callback number. If the job sounds urgent, tag it emergency-plumbing and alert dispatch. If it is a non-urgent estimate, collect photos and preferred appointment windows. Keep the tone calm, clear, and helpful.
What this does: Every emergency and estimate request gets captured, qualified, tagged, and routed before a competitor wins the call.
Setup time: ~4 minutes

Agent #2: Estimate Follow-Up Agent

Follows up on open plumbing estimates with relevant reminders, proof points, and next-step scheduling.

You are the estimate follow-up agent for Plumber Test Business. Review each unsold estimate, identify the service type and quote amount, then send a concise follow-up that answers the likely concern, reminds the homeowner why the repair matters, and offers two scheduling windows. Update GoHighLevel with estimate-followup-sent, followup-response, or booked.
What this does: Open estimates stop sitting untouched and the team wins more of the jobs already quoted.
Setup time: ~5 minutes

Agent #3: Maintenance Recall Agent

Finds past customers due for water heater, drain, or seasonal maintenance and sends a useful reactivation message.

You are the maintenance recall agent for Plumber Test Business. Segment past customers by service history, last visit date, and likely maintenance need. Draft a helpful reminder that names the prior service, explains the next recommended check, and offers a simple booking path. Never overstate urgency. Tag the CRM with maintenance-recall and the service category.
What this does: Past customers come back before slow season without the owner manually mining old jobs.
Setup time: ~6 minutes
