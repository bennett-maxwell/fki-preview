At **$1.5M in revenue on a 4-person team**, the limit isn't demand. It's hours. This blueprint is built around that one outcome. It maps AI agents directly onto the work Versa already does, so the business keeps moving while you step up to lead it.

## 5. Your 6 AI Agents

Six agents, mapped to the workflows already draining Versa's week. Each one runs the repeatable work so your 4-person team operates like a much larger one.

### 1. Lead-Gen Agent
Works your eight offer lines — funding, merchant services, tax credits, Section 125, consumer financing, CC stacking, debt settlement, and SBA — and keeps the top of the funnel full without you manually sourcing every prospect. Built around your current flow of roughly **30 leads/month**, designed to lift that number, not just maintain it.

### 2. Follow-Up Agent
Instant response and structured follow-up on every inbound funding and merchant-services lead, so no opportunity goes cold while you're heads-down on another deal. Speed-to-lead is one of your named drain points — this agent closes that gap automatically.

### 3. Email-Triage Agent
Reads, sorts, and drafts replies across your inbox so client questions, partner messages, and new inquiries get routed and answered fast — without you living in your email.

### 4. Content Agent
Turns one **Versa Business Tips Podcast** episode into a full content engine: clips, show notes, LinkedIn posts, an email newsletter, and lead magnets — automatically. The content drain becomes a content flywheel.

### 5. Offer-Routing Agent
Qualifies each lead and routes them to the right Versa offer — funding vs. merchant services vs. tax credits vs. SBA vs. financing — and arms your **AI Systems Affiliate Program** partners with the right materials to recruit, onboard, and sell. The system matches the prospect to the product instead of you triaging it by hand.

### 6. Admin & Reporting Agent
Handles the back-office work — pipeline updates, status tracking, and the weekly reporting that tells you what's working — so the numbers come to you instead of you digging for them.

## 6. The Time-Drain Gaps AI Closes

Right now, the company spends **140 hours a week** and you spend **24 of your own hours** on work that repeats. Here are the biggest gaps this blueprint closes:

1. **Lead generation** — chasing prospects by hand across eight offer lines instead of a system keeping the funnel full.
2. **Follow-up** — leads going cold because there's no instant, consistent response on every inbound inquiry.
3. **Email** — hours lost to triage, sorting, and replies that an agent can draft and route for you.
4. **Content** — every podcast episode, post, and newsletter built manually instead of generated once and repurposed everywhere.
5. **Offer routing & reporting** — manually matching leads to the right product and assembling the numbers, instead of the system doing both.

Close these five and the math changes: the **24 hours a week** you spend running workflows becomes time to **hire, train, and scale** the team that grows Versa past where four people can take it.
