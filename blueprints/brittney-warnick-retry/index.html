The Opportunity

Three places AI changes the economics of Britt Warnick Designs

An interior design studio at your stage has specific AI leverage points that bigger firms cannot move on quickly. These are yours to capture now.

01

Inquiry Response Speed

Design inquiries close at the highest rate when the firm responds within the first hour. AI auto-replies to every form fill and DM, captures style preferences and budget, and books the consultation before the prospect contacts a competing designer.

02

Proposal Drafting Automation

Custom proposals are where designer hours disappear. AI drafts the full scope of work, timeline, mood-board summary and investment ranges from your intake notes, leaving you to refine instead of starting from scratch each time.

03

Past-Client Reactivation

Most interior design revenue lives in the existing client base — second rooms, second homes, referrals. AI segments your past-client list by project age and trigger event, fires personalized check-ins that turn one project into three.
