The Opportunity

Three places AI changes the economics of Mahana Fresh

A fast-casual restaurant at your volume has specific AI leverage points that national chains cannot move on quickly. These are yours to capture now.

01

Catering Inquiry Speed

Catering leads convert 6x higher when quoted within 10 minutes. AI auto-replies to every form fill, gathers headcount and date, and locks the deposit before the customer calls a competitor.

02

Loyalty Re-Engagement

Most lapsed customers never come back unprompted. AI segments your loyalty list by visit decay, fires personalized win-back offers tied to local weather and pay-day cycles.

03

Review and Reputation Engine

Happy guests rarely leave reviews on their own. AI triggers Google review asks at peak satisfaction moments and routes any 1-3 star signal to a manager before it goes public.
