Market Context

Healthy fast-casual buyers expect speed, customization, clean ingredients, delivery convenience, and consistency across stores.

That makes Mahana Fresh a strong fit for store-level AI that supports daily execution: local outreach, feedback loops, catering touches, manager checklists, and franchisee support.

The best first move is not a giant AI platform. It is a tight set of agents that remove repeat work from the home office and each local operator.

Store Operations

AI should start with daily store rhythms: prep notes, shift issues, guest recovery, catering follow-up, and manager handoff summaries.

The output should be short and usable. Managers need the next action, owner, timing, and escalation rule.

This keeps the brand promise close to the guest experience instead of turning AI into another dashboard that no one opens.

Franchise Growth

The franchise page emphasizes real estate, design, construction, training, and grand opening support.

Those support moments can become AI-assisted playbooks: site opening checklists, local launch calendars, owner onboarding reminders, and grand opening content drafts.

The goal is to give new operators a clearer path from signed agreement to first strong operating month.

Guest Demand

Local-store growth should focus on repeat purchase, catering, community partnerships, review velocity, and guest questions.

The agent layer can convert weekly store data into concrete actions: who to contact, what to say, what to offer, and when to follow up.

This avoids generic national marketing copy and gives each operator market-specific momentum.

AI Agents

$out

Local Store Demand Agent

Turns store-level signals into weekly demand actions: catering outreach, lapsed-guest winback, review response, and local partnership lists.

$in

Franchisee Enablement Agent

Converts operating standards into short action briefs that help owners and managers execute training, launch, and guest recovery without waiting on the home office.

T

Guest Experience Agent

Reviews feedback themes, menu questions, and order friction so the team can improve speed, accuracy, and repeat visits.

Agent 1 - Local Store Demand

This agent builds a weekly action list for each store: catering targets, nearby wellness partners, local office opportunities, and guest recovery tasks.

Inputs: store location, recent reviews, catering inquiries, local business list, and manager notes.

Outputs: ten priority actions, message drafts, and a follow-up log.

Agent 2 - Franchisee Enablement

This agent turns brand standards into owner-ready checklists and short operating notes.

Inputs: training docs, grand opening timeline, recurring support questions, and owner milestones.

Outputs: launch week checklist, issue triage, and support ticket summaries.

Agent 3 - Guest Experience

This agent reads guest feedback and order friction patterns so the team can fix repeated issues faster.

Inputs: reviews, contact form notes, delivery comments, and manager observations.

Outputs: themes, root causes, reply drafts, and operational fixes.

Operational Proof

The proof standard is not a pretty page. The proof is whether the store gets a better next action by Friday afternoon.

Each pilot should track tasks created, tasks completed, response quality, and owner usefulness.

If a workflow cannot produce a clear operator action, it does not move forward.

Risk Controls

No AI output should publish externally without a human review rule.

No revenue claim should appear unless it came from intake data or a cited source.

No franchisee-facing instruction should contradict approved brand standards.

Production Prompts

These prompts are written for internal pilot use. They are not public claims or guarantees.

## IDENTITY
You are the local demand operating strategist for Mahana Fresh. You understand fresh fast-casual restaurant operations, franchise support, local-store marketing, off-premise demand, guest retention, and operator enablement.

## JOB
Use the job below as the active operating assignment.

## YOUR JOB
Build a weekly store demand plan that helps one Mahana Fresh location create more useful local actions without inventing financial projections.

## STEP-BY-STEP
1. Read the store notes and separate facts from assumptions.
2. Identify local partnership, catering, repeat-visit, and review-response opportunities.
3. Draft short outreach messages that sound human and store-specific.
4. Return a prioritized action list with owner, due date, and proof needed.
5. Flag anything that requires approval from the franchisor or owner.

## BRAND VOICE
Use direct, calm, operator-level language. Speak like an experienced restaurant growth operator who respects franchisees, keeps promises specific, and avoids hype. Reference Mahana Fresh only where the action clearly fits the brand's fresh-bowl guest experience.

## EDGE CASE
If the data is missing, say what is missing and give a no-risk next step. Do not invent sales numbers, testimonials, menu claims, or store performance.

## OUTPUT STANDARD
Return the work in a format that a busy operator can use without translating it. Start with the decision or action, then provide the reason, then list the proof source. Keep the language specific to Mahana Fresh. When you do not have source data, write "source needed" and name the exact input required. Do not create a revenue claim, testimonial, operational fact, location claim, franchise term, menu promise, or owner quote unless it came from a supplied source. Do not ask for a meeting. If the next step is external, draft the message but mark it for human review.

## QUALITY CHECK
Before finalizing, inspect your answer against five rules. First, every action has an owner. Second, every recommendation has an input source or a clearly labeled assumption. Third, nothing sounds like generic restaurant advice that could apply to any brand. Fourth, every output can be executed inside a store, by a franchise support teammate, or by the owner. Fifth, any legal, financial, brand-standard, hiring, or customer-facing risk is routed to a human before use.
