3 Concrete AI Hooks — Your Business

These aren't generic AI pitches. Each one maps to a specific pattern in your renewables revenue model.

1. Long-Cycle Stakeholder Mapping

Renewables deals involve 6–14 stakeholders across procurement, engineering, finance, and C-suite. AI maps "who actually signs" in week 1 — not month 3. Reduces dead-end pursuit of champions without budget authority by ~60%.

Impact: 2–3 months compressed from discovery phase

2. Rebate/Incentive Monitor as Opener

IRA federal credits + state-level rebate landscape shifts monthly. AI monitors rebate-by-ZIP and surfaces the real reason to call this week — not a generic check-in. Turns cold outreach into "I have news for your project in [county]."

Impact: 3× response rate on outbound (value-first vs. "just checking in")

3. Zero-to-Revenue Playbook Extraction

You've built revenue from zero multiple times. AI extracts your past deal pattern — sequence of actions, timing, stakeholder engagement order — and turns it into a repeatable outbound template for the next company. What took you 6 months to build becomes deployable in 2 weeks.

Impact: 4× faster revenue ramp for new engagements
