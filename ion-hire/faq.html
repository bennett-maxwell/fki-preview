FAQ for people considering the job

Honest answers for 100% commission door-to-door.

If you need a guaranteed hourly check, this is not the role. If you want a skill that compounds, keep reading.

Is this hourly or commission?

Commission only. Door-to-door solar setter. If you thought this was a W-2 hourly office job, stop here. We will not sell you a base that does not exist.

Why would anyone take a job with no base pay?

Because the skill is the product. You learn to start conversations with strangers, handle rejection, and run your own day. Money can follow that skill. We will not invent a weekly number. People who last in this work usually stay for the skill and the time control, not a poster salary.

What skill do I actually walk away with?

Talking to people who did not ask to talk to you. Reading a door. Recovering from no. Showing up when the weather is bad. That stack is why door-to-door people later sell, manage, or start companies. Bennett Maxwell (who is building this recruiting system with Adam Webb) has said on record he got into door-to-door about ten years ago and started a solar company in Utah in 2020. That is origin, not an ION pay claim.

Do I need sales experience?

You need a car, a commute, and a stomach for commission-only knocking. Experience helps. Honesty about the four screens matters more. The virtual seminar is where ION trains the pitch — we do not pretend the phone screen is the training.

Do I need my own car?

Yes. A working vehicle you own, not borrowed. No car = not this job. We ask this first so we do not waste your time.

Where do I work? Is it remote?

Not remote. Daily commute to the ION office you are assigned. ION’s HQ is Orem, Utah. Exact office list is owner-verify — we will not invent cities. If you cannot get to the office every workday, this is a no.

What is a setter vs a closer?

A setter knocks and books the appointment. A closer runs the in-home. This one-pager is for the setter role the recruiting agent is screening. Do not mix the jobs in ads.

How much will I make?

We do not quote pay. Third-party boards that print USD 60k–USD 250k or “hourly base” are not ION facts. Anyone who needs a guaranteed number should not take this role. Anyone who wants the skill and is willing to work commission-only can go to the seminar and hear ION’s own explanation.

What happens after I apply?

Phone screen. Four hard checks. If you pass, we sell the job honestly and book a virtual seminar. KPI for the AI recruiter is apply → seminar scheduled, not “you are hired.” Field interview and knocking come after the seminar. Dates and offices are ION’s, not invented here.

Felonies?

No felonies in the last 10 years. Asked on the call. State wording can be rephrased if Adam names a state that requires it. Do not put this on a public Indeed form until that legal check is done.

Can I try it part time?

Owner-verify. Do not promise nights-only, weekends-only, or “see how it goes” until Adam says so. Default copy: this is a daily field job.

Who is ION Solar?

Residential solar company. Public site (2026-08-19): founded 2013, 85,000+ installs, Top 5 U.S. residential installer, 4.5+ stars from 7,000+ reviews. Orem HQ. Not a franchise offering. Do not use FDD language.

Why ION instead of a random door shop?

Named brand, named HQ, named product (homeowner solar), and a real next step (virtual seminar) instead of a parking-lot pitch. Still commission-only. Still hard. The brand is the filter, not a pillow.

Will an AI call me?

That is the system being built. It is not live on Indeed until ION has paid, given HighLevel login, given 20 scored recordings, and written the 30-day human baseline. Until then, humans run the queue. Do not tell candidates the bot is already calling.

What if I fail a screen?

We stop. We do not book you to hit a number. Booked is not good if the screen was skipped.
