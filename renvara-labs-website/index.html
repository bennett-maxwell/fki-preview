How we test

The stated seven-point testing program

In short

Renvara Labs states that every peptide it offers goes through a seven-point analytical and microbiological testing program performed in Utah: HPLC/UPLC, mass spectrometry, identity or sequence, LAL/BET endotoxin, elemental impurities, residual solvents and sterility. The six public lot reports today cover purity, identity and quantitation by UPLC/MS; the other results are not published.

  1. HPLC / UPLCPurity and related impurities
  2. Mass spectrometryMolecular mass and identity
  3. Identity / sequenceIntended peptide confirmation
  4. LAL / BET endotoxinBacterial endotoxins
  5. Elemental impuritiesHeavy-metal screening
  6. Residual solventsManufacturing-solvent screening
  7. Sterility testingViable microbial contamination

Quick answers

Which report applies to my vial?

Use the report with the same product, strength and lot number as your vial. A report for a different lot does not describe the lot you received. The COA library lists six public lot reports as of 2026-10-10.

What do the public COAs cover today?

The six public reports in the COA library are listed as UPLC/MS purity, identity and quantitation, all analyzed on 2026-09-16 by Horizon Analytical. They are not endotoxin, sterility, elemental-impurity or residual-solvent reports.

How much is shipping?

For U.S. orders: DHL Ground is $12.99 under $150 and free at $150 and above. FedEx 2Day is $19.99 under $150, $6.99 from $150 to $199.99, and free at $200 and above. Next Day is $32.99 at every order value.

What does research use only mean?

Products are supplied for laboratory research only. They are not for human or veterinary use, diagnostic use or use in food, and they have not been evaluated by the FDA. Testing describes the sample tested and does not make a product suitable for any other use.
