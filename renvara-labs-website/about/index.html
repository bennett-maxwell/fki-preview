Who we are

Entity summary

Renvara Labs is the trade name of Oak Canyon Bridge LLC, which supplies research peptides, peptide blends and laboratory materials to qualified adult researchers. It publishes lot-specific certificates of analysis in a public COA library, states a seven-point testing program performed in Utah, and lists a PO Box in Lehi, Utah and a support email for contact.

The company focuses on clear product information, lot traceability and accessible quality documentation, so that a researcher can match a certificate to a vial and know what the certificate does and does not cover.

Research use only
