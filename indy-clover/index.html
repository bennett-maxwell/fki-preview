Own a resale retail store with the buildout handled up front.

Indy Clover is a curated resale retail concept. Sellers rent booth space, shoppers buy secondhand, and the franchisor's affiliate assembles the opening package — fixtures, signage, technology and initial inventory — as a single Opening Order before you open.

How the model works

Booth-based resale floor

Individual sellers rent booth space inside your store. Your team runs the floor, the point-of-sale and the customer experience.

Opening Order handled by the franchisor's affiliate

ICFFE, LLC procures the required signage, booths, shelving, racks, mirrors, furniture, fixtures, computer and office equipment, software, custom paint and décor. (Item 5)

Inventory to open included

The Opening Order includes required inventory to open and operate for 3–6 months. (Item 5)

Training at company headquarters

Mandatory initial training takes place at the franchisor's headquarters in Lindon, Utah, and covers operational standards, business management and initial seller recruiting. (Item 5)

Terms disclosed in the FDD

  • Multi-unit: the initial franchise fee for the second and third franchises is reduced to $30,000 per franchise, under a Multi-Unit Addendum. To qualify for the reduced fee you must pay the entire initial franchise fee upfront for each franchise when you sign the first franchise agreement and Multi-Unit Addendum. (Item 5)
  • Opening deadline: you must open the franchise within 180 days after the date of the Franchise Agreement. (Item 5)
  • When fees are due: the Initial Franchise Fee and the Training Fee are each due at the time the Franchise Agreement is executed. (Item 5)
  • Refunds: the Initial Franchise Fee, the Training Fee and the amount paid for initial purchases are not refundable in whole or in part, other than as described in Item 5. (Item 5)
  • System size: as of June 30, 2025 there were 17 Indy Clover store locations, 3 of which were owned and operated by the franchisor's affiliates. (Item 19)
