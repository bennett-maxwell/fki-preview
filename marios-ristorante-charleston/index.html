Meet Chef Mario

Third-generation Calabrian — cooking Charleston since 2004

M

Chef Mario Greco was raised in his grandmother's kitchen in Reggio Calabria, where Sunday meant fresh pasta on every counter and the wood oven never went cold. After training in Rome and Bologna, Mario brought his family's recipes to Charleston in 2004 — and has been hand-rolling pasta on King Street ever since.

What that means for you: every noodle, every sauce, every loaf of focaccia is made in-house from imported Italian flour, San Marzano tomatoes, and the same recipes Mario's grandmother wrote down by hand.

FAQ

Do you take reservations?

Yes — we strongly recommend reservations Thursday through Saturday. Use the form above or call us directly.

Are you walk-in friendly?

Always. We hold bar seating for walk-ins every night, and Sunday–Wednesday usually have full table availability.

Do you have gluten-free pasta?

Yes. Any pasta dish can be prepared with house-made gluten-free fettuccine ($3 add).

Is parking available?

Street parking is free after 6 PM Sun–Thu. The Charleston Place garage (2 blocks north) validates with dinner receipt.

Can you accommodate large parties?

Yes — our private dining room seats up to 24. Call for prix-fixe pricing.
