FAQ

Questions, answered.

Clear answers before a fit call. No jargon theater.

What does Advaita actually sell?

A done-for-you client-acquisition system: paid ads, conversion funnel, follow-up automation in CRMX/GoHighLevel, and AI agents that respond/qualify/book.

Is this a software product or an agency?

A marketing agency that runs on AI. You get the horsepower of a growth team without building the stack alone — and without paying traditional agency bloat.

Which businesses are a fit?

High-ticket home services with in-home consultative sales: water treatment/filtration (launch wedge), HVAC, solar, roofing/exterior remodel.

How fast can we go live?

Measured in days for core system wiring when inputs are ready — not multi-month “strategy decks” with no pipeline. Exact timing depends on ad accounts, offers, and CRM access.

Do you guarantee revenue numbers?

No. We do not fabricate metrics. Case figures on marketing materials are labeled and require owner confirmation before use in ads or contracts.

Where do SMS ads and compliance fit?

Outbound SMS requires A2P/10DLC brand & campaign registration with carriers. That path can include human identity gates. We package the steps; carriers decide final approval.
