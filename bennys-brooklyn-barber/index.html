Frequently Asked

 

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome but Saturdays book solid. We recommend reserving a chair through the booking widget above to lock in your barber.

Can I request a specific barber?

Always. Each of our four barbers has a specialty — Benny for classic scissor work, Diego for skin fades, Jamal for shaves and beard sculpting, Marco for razor-line classics.

How does the Benny's Club membership work?

$79/month gets you two haircuts with any barber, 10% off shaves and retail, priority booking, and a complimentary drink. No contract — cancel anytime.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel or reschedule up to 4 hours before your appointment with no charge. Within 4 hours, we charge 50% of the service fee.

Do you do hot-towel straight-razor shaves?

Yes — Jamal is our specialist. Steam, hot towel, traditional straight razor, beard oil finish. 40 minutes. $50.
