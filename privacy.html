Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-09

Franchise KI ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page is the short version. We don't sell your data. We don't share your email. We don't build advertising profiles on you.

What we collect

  • Information you give us when you take the AI Audit (name, email, phone, capital, timeline, operator type, industries, problem statement).
  • Standard server logs (IP, user agent, referrer) for security and debugging.
  • Aggregate analytics (page views, time on page) — never linked to you personally.

What we do with it

  • Generate your personalized franchise audit podcast.
  • Email you the audit and follow-up only if you reply or book a call.
  • Match you with franchisors only when you've explicitly approved the introduction.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your contact information.
  • Add you to mailing lists you didn't opt into.
  • Share your information with franchisors before you say yes.

Your rights

Email bennett@franchiseki.com to access, correct, or delete your data. We respond within 5 business days.

Cookies

We use first-party cookies for analytics. No third-party advertising cookies.
