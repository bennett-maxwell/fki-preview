Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-23

Franchise KI ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page is the short version. We don't sell your data. We don't share your email. We don't build advertising profiles on you.

What we collect

  • Information you give us when you take the AI Qualifier and request your personalized AI Blueprint (name, business name, email, phone, monthly leads, average deal value, close rate, response time, team size, marketing spend, biggest operational drain).
  • Standard server logs (IP address, user agent, referrer) for security and debugging.
  • Aggregate analytics (page views, time on page) — never linked to you personally.

What we do with it

  • Generate your personalized AI Blueprint — a custom written report and audio podcast modeled on your specific numbers.
  • Email your Blueprint to the address you provide so you have it on file.
  • Send a short, optional follow-up only if you want to discuss deployment. You can opt out at any time with one click.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your contact information.
  • Add you to mailing lists you didn't opt into.
  • Share your information with third parties without your explicit consent.

Your rights

Email contact@franchiseki.com to access, correct, or delete your data. We respond within 5 business days.

Cookies

We use first-party cookies for analytics. No third-party advertising cookies.

Contact
