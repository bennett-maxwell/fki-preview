How Voice Accuracy Works

Calibration Happens Before Launch — Never "Learns Over Time"

The most common failure mode in AI deployment is a generic voice that sounds like nobody's brand. Advaita's calibration process exists to eliminate that failure mode before the first message ever sends.

1
Source material collected up front
Real call transcripts, recordings, and published content — not a brand questionnaire. Every voice rule in the calibration profile must trace to something the client actually said or wrote.
2
Calibration profile built and scored before launch
Tone summary, sentence-structure patterns, banned phrases, decision logic for the top workflows, and escalation rules — each section carries a confidence score. Any section below threshold gets a follow-up call before that employee goes live.
3
Client reviews and approves the profile
Nothing goes live on an assumption. The client owner signs off on the calibration profile itself before any AI Employee is connected to a live channel.
4
90% Day-1 accuracy is the standard, not the ceiling
Because the voice was built from an intelligence audit up front, employees launch accurate from message one. Day 14 and Day 30 checks refine further based on real production data — that is refinement, not the system learning what the brand sounds like from scratch.
5
Drift is caught, never let it compound
Real production output is compared line by line against the calibration profile at Day 14. Any output scoring outside the profile's rules is flagged — if drift shows up in more than 10% of the sample, a recalibration call happens that week, not at the next scheduled review.

Trust By Design

Approval Gates — A Human Approves Every External Send

AI Employees draft. Humans approve. This is not a limitation Advaita is working around — it is how trust is built in phases while the system proves itself on real client data.

A
Draft-then-approve is the default for every client-facing message
Speed-to-Lead responses, retention check-ins, content posts, and reporting digests are all drafted by the AI Employee and posted to Slack (or staged in GoHighLevel) for the named approver before anything sends externally.
B
One named approver per workflow, confirmed at kickoff
No ambiguity about who signs off. The kickoff checklist names the approver for each employee category; that person can delegate the role after Day 30 once the calibration profile has proven itself in production.
C
High-risk categories carry a hard compliance escalation rule
Anything touching legal, franchise-registration, financial guarantees, or regulated claims routes to a human before drafting even completes — regardless of how confident the calibration profile is otherwise.
D
Escalation over guessing, always
Every AI Employee's decision logic includes explicit escalation rules: angry or ambiguous inquiries, price-first questions, and anything outside a documented workflow route to a human rather than a best-guess response.

After Day 30

Escalation, Support & Month-2+ Expansion

Day 30 is a calibration milestone, not a handoff-and-disappear moment. Advaita stays engaged as the client's AI operations team.

What happens if something breaks or an employee misfires?
Any flagged drift, error, or edge case an AI Employee cannot resolve routes to a human immediately — either the client's named approver for day-to-day items, or the Advaita Implementation Lead for anything tool- or configuration-related. Nothing sits unresolved waiting for a scheduled review.
Who do we contact for support after rollout?
The Advaita Implementation Lead remains the primary point of contact through Day 30. After calibration, ongoing support routes through Advaita's standard support channel, with response times matching the client's plan tier.
What does Month 2 and beyond look like?
With the core six employees calibrated and proven on real data, expansion moves to the next-highest-leverage workflow identified during the Day 30 review — never added speculatively. Common Month 2+ expansions include deeper pipeline automation, additional content formats, or extending the Retention & Reporting Employee's account coverage as the client's book of business grows.
Do approval gates loosen over time?
Only by explicit client decision. As the calibration profile proves itself in production — measured, not assumed — the client owner can choose to expand which categories of message auto-send versus route for approval. Advaita never removes an approval gate unilaterally.

What Comes Next

Month 2+ Expansion Paths

Sequencing, not limits. These are the categories Advaita holds for later phases by design — proven on itself first before ever being offered to a client on Day 1.

Deeper pipeline automation
Once Speed-to-Lead performance is proven over 30 days, additional pipeline stages and follow-up sequences can be automated with the same calibrated voice.
Expanded content formats

Expanded approval autonomy
