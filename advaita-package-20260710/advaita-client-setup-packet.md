## 3. Access and Credentials Checklist

This section lists what the client (Advaita AI, in this exercise) needs to provide so the AI Employees can be configured and calibrated. **No actual credentials, tokens, passwords, or API keys are recorded anywhere in this packet.** Credentials are collected directly from the client in a live session and stored only in the client's own secure configuration — never printed, pasted, or logged in a shared document.

### Access Needed From the Client
- [ ] **GoHighLevel (GHL) access** — location-level API key or admin access, so the Speed-to-Lead Response Employee can connect to the lead pipeline and update stages.
- [ ] **Call transcripts / call recordings** — real, representative samples of how the client actually talks to customers. This is the single most important input: voice calibration is built from this material, not guessed.
- [ ] **Published content samples** — website copy, past emails, social posts, anything already public in the client's voice.
- [ ] **Email platform access** — whichever platform is in use (Gmail, Outlook, Zoho, etc.), for the employees that touch inbound/outbound communication.
- [ ] **Calendar access** — if scheduling or meeting-prep employees are in scope.
- [ ] **Accounting platform access** (e.g., QuickBooks) — required only for the Finance Pulse Employee.
- [ ] **CRM/pipeline structure** — current pipeline stages, so routing logic matches how the business actually operates today.
- [ ] **Brand/voice preferences** — any explicit dos and don'ts the client already knows about their own voice, in addition to what gets extracted from transcripts.

### What This Packet Will Never Contain
- Raw API keys, OAuth tokens, passwords, or session cookies.
- Screenshots or text of actual credential fields.
- Any live customer data beyond what is needed for calibration, and only in the client's own secure environment.

Missing credentials are never treated as a reason to bypass approval gates or improvise access. If something is missing, the relevant employee stays blocked until the client provides it.

## 6. Calibration Process

Voice and workflow calibration is **built before launch**, not learned live on real customers. This is a hard rule, not a preference.

**How it works:**
1. The Client Onboarding & Calibration Employee ingests real call transcripts, recordings, and published content provided by the client.
2. Every voice trait written into the calibration profile must trace back to a specific transcript or piece of content the client actually provided. If a trait cannot be verified from real source material, the profile marks it "unknown — confirm with client" rather than guessing.
3. The result: the AI sounds like the client from Day 1, targeting **90% accuracy on Day 1** — because the voice work happened up front, not through trial and error in front of customers.
4. After launch, adjustments are treated as calibration refinements, not as "fixing a broken system." Fine-tuning against real production conversations in the first 30 days is expected and normal.
5. A week-one drift check compares production output against the calibration profile to confirm accuracy is holding.

This process applies to every AI Employee that generates client-facing language, not just the Speed-to-Lead Response Employee.

## 7. Approval Gates

Human approval is a permanent structural feature of this system, not a temporary phase.

- **Every external send requires human approval.** The AI drafts client-facing messages; a human approves before anything leaves the building.
- **Autonomous pricing is out of scope for this phase.** The AI can model numbers; pricing decisions stay with leadership.
- **Human strategy calls are not replaced.** The AI preps briefs and handles admin around a call; the strategic conversation itself stays human.
- **No generic, uncalibrated chatbots are deployed.** Every AI Employee that talks to a customer is calibrated first — there is no off-the-shelf FAQ bot shortcut in this system.

These are described as sequencing decisions, not permanent limits — later phases can expand autonomy, but only with explicit client approval logged at the time of the change.

## 9. FAQ

**Is this just a chatbot?**
No. Each AI Employee is a calibrated, workflow-specific system — Speed-to-Lead Response, Onboarding & Calibration, Blueprint Builder, Content Engine, Retention & Reporting, and Finance Pulse — each mapped to a real business process, not a generic FAQ bot.

**Will the AI sound like a robot and embarrass my brand?**
The calibration process exists specifically to prevent this. Voice is built from real transcripts and content before launch, targeting 90% accuracy on Day 1. Traits that can't be verified from real source material are flagged, not guessed.

**I'm not technical. Can I actually run this?**
Yes. The team walkthrough takes about 30 minutes and requires no technical background. The AI Employees connect to tools the business already uses rather than requiring a new stack.

**What if the AI sends something wrong to a customer?**
It cannot send anything on its own. Every client-facing message is drafted by AI and approved by a human before it goes out. This approval gate does not go away as trust builds — it is a permanent design choice.

**How do I know the ROI numbers aren't inflated?**
The calculator does not pre-fill or invent numbers. The client enters their own average customer value, lead volume, close rate, and admin hours. The lift math is based on published research (McKinsey, HBR) with a conservative preset running at half the published benchmark. The page states plainly that results vary and nothing is guaranteed. Individual results vary. See Item 19 of the applicable FDD.

**What does this cost?**
Fees depend on scope. Pricing is discussed on a call after walking the full system — it is never quoted in a first automated response.

**What data do you need from us, and is it safe?**
Call transcripts, published content, and access to the specific tools each AI Employee touches (see Section 3). No credentials are ever stored in shared documents; access is configured directly in the client's own secure environment.

**Can we turn off or pause an AI Employee at any time?**
Yes. Any AI Employee can be paused by the client at any time; pausing does not require a reason.
