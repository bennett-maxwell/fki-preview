Common Questions

Everything you want to know about San Diego water.

Real answers — no fluff, no upsell in disguise.

San Diego water consistently tests at 250–500 mg/L (15–29 grains per gallon) — classified "very hard" by the Water Quality Association. MWD supply to South San Diego averages around 300–420 ppm. That hardness deposits scale inside your water heater, restricts flow in pipes over time, and causes the chalky buildup you see on faucets and showerheads.
A professionally installed whole-house water softener in San Diego typically runs $1,500–$3,800 depending on home size, water hardness, and system brand. We give you one transparent quote before touching anything — no surprise upcharges on install day. The free water test tells us exactly what size you need so we don't oversell you.
A water softener removes hardness minerals (calcium and magnesium) through ion exchange — it prevents scale buildup on appliances, fixtures, and pipes. A water filter removes contaminants like chlorine, sediment, and odors. Many San Diego homes benefit from both: a whole-house softener protecting your plumbing and appliances, plus an under-sink reverse osmosis system for drinking water. We'll tell you honestly whether you need one, both, or neither.
Most whole-house water softener and RO system installations are completed in 2–4 hours in a single visit. We do same-day installations throughout San Diego and Riverside counties. We show up on time, work clean, and leave your home cleaner than we found it.
San Diego tap water meets all federal safety standards — it won't make you sick. But its high hardness (300–500 ppm) and chlorine treatment make most residents prefer filtered water for drinking and cooking. A 5-stage reverse osmosis system removes 99%+ of contaminants and produces water that tastes better than anything you can buy — and costs far less than bottled water over time.
No contracts, no subscriptions, no monthly fees. You buy the system, we install it, it's yours. We offer optional annual maintenance plans for filter swaps — but those are completely optional and there's no commitment. The equipment comes with the manufacturer's warranty and our 100% satisfaction guarantee.
