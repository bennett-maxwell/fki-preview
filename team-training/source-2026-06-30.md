## How AI Thinks (Agentic 20%)
2-3 simple ideas in plain words.

1. Idea: Sub-agents (small helpers that split up the work).
   - Plain explanation: instead of one helper doing everything, the AI can send out little helpers to each do one part, then bring it all back. Claude Code now even lets a helper make its own helpers, a few layers deep.
   - When to use it today: on a big job, say "break this into parts and have helpers do each part."
   - Exact words to say: "split this up and run the parts at the same time."
2. Idea: The self-check loop (the helper checks its own work before saying done).
   - Plain explanation: a good helper does the work, then re-reads it against what you actually asked, fixes the gaps, THEN reports. Our nightly audit did exactly this — found 8, fixed 4 itself.
   - When to use it today: say "check your own work against what I asked before you tell me it's done."
   - Exact words to say: "self-check this before you call it done."
3. Idea: The troubleshoot path (if it's stuck, say THIS).
   - Plain explanation: when something jams, escalate in steps instead of giving up.
   - Exact words to say: first "troubleshoot this," then if still stuck "council this," then if still stuck post in the team chat.
