## Top Issues Seen
1. Two days in a row the daily audio did not finish — Google's free audio limit and an expired login blocked the recording.
2. The login that makes the audio kept timing out, so the helper that runs by itself could not finish the job.
3. When a login breaks, the fix has to come from a live, logged-in run — a background timer alone could not log back in.
4. A bad saved password was quietly blocking the website push until it got cleared and the right login took over.
5. Self-sent email (from you, to you) gets auto-marked read and hidden, so reports were landing where nobody saw them.

DEEP DIVE — Issue 1 (audio did not finish):
- Issue: The daily recording failed Tuesday and the Friday before.
- Example/evidence: The logs show "Google daily audio limit" and "login expired," so the audio never built.
- Root cause: Two things — a daily cap on free audio, and a login that times out and needs a fresh, signed-in session.
- Correct behavior: Make the audio from a live run that is already signed in, not from a background timer that cannot log back in. Today's episode was made that way and it worked.
- Exact words to say next time: "The audio login looks expired — refresh it from a signed-in run, then try the recording again."

DEEP DIVE — Issue 5 (hidden self-email):
- Issue: A report you email from yourself to yourself gets marked read and filed away, so you miss it.
- Root cause: Email rules hide mail that comes from your own address.
- Correct behavior: Have the AI send from the team AI mailbox, not from your own address to your own address.
- Exact words to say next time: "Send this from the AI team email, not from me to me, so it shows up unread in my inbox."

## How AI Thinks (Agentic 20%)
Idea 1 — Helpers that split the work (sub-agents):
- Plain explanation: One AI can hand pieces of a big job to smaller helper AIs, who all work at the same time, then report back.
- When to use it today: When a job feels too big for one go — ask the AI to break it up.
- Exact words to say: "Break this into parts and run them at the same time."

Idea 2 — The AI checks its own work (self-audit loop):
- Plain explanation: Before the AI says "done," a separate checker looks at the work to make sure it really happened. Our watchdogs do this all day.
- When to use it today: Any time the answer matters — ask for proof.
- Exact words to say: "Check your own work and show me the proof before you say it is done."

Idea 3 — Don't say done, show proof:
- Plain explanation: "Done" is just a word. A link, a receipt, or a file is real proof.
- When to use it today: Every single task.
- Exact words to say: "Don't tell me it is done — show me the link."
