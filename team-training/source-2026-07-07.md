## How AI Thinks (Agentic 20%)
- Idea: Sub-agents (small helpers splitting up the work). Plain explanation: for a big job, your AI can send out little helper AIs, each doing one part, then bring the answers back together. When to use it today: when a task has many separate parts (research + write + check). Exact words: "use a bigger workflow and split this into parts."
- Idea: Self-audit loop (the helper checks itself before saying done). Plain explanation: a good AI does not just say "done" — it re-reads its own work against the goal, finds gaps, and fixes them first. When to use it today: any time you want proof, not a promise. Exact words: "check your own work and show me proof before you say it's done."
- Idea: When a stop-sign is a false alarm. Plain explanation: safety guards sometimes stop on a scary WORD, not a real risk. The smart move is to ask "is the actual action risky, or just the wording?" When to use it today: when the AI freezes on something clearly harmless. Exact words: "is this actually risky, or just the wording? If it's reversible, keep going."
