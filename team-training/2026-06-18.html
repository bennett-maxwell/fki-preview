Your first skill in 10 minutes

Pick one task you repeat at work. Here's how to turn it into a saved tool (called a "skill") so the AI runs it for you every time:

  1. Open Claude Code — the app where you chat with your AI helper (you type, it does the work).
  2. Do the task once with the AI in plain words.
  3. Say: Turn this into a skill so I can do it again with one sentence.
  4. Next time, just say the skill name. The AI runs the whole thing.
How AI thinks (plain words)

The connector is the key — not the AI
The AI is only as powerful as the systems it can log in to. When GHL or QuickBooks disconnects, it is not the AI's fault — it is a login key that expired. Like a car key fob with a dead battery. The car still works. Just reconnect it.

Audit-only mode is your safe test drive
Before the AI does anything that touches real customers or money, ask for audit-only mode. It reads and analyzes without changing anything.
Say: "Run this in audit-only mode — no sends, no changes."

Done = a link you can click
An AI that says "task complete" with no link is like a delivery driver who says "I delivered it" with no tracking number. Always ask.
Say: "Give me the proof link."
