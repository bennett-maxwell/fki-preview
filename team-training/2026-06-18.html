How AI thinks

•The connector is the key, not the AI: the AI is only as powerful as the systems it can log into. A dead GHL or QuickBooks connection is a login-key problem, not an AI problem — like a dead key-fob battery, the car still works, just reconnect it. Say: "Reconnect the GHL connector" or "Reconnect QuickBooks."
•Done means a link you can click: an AI saying "task complete" with no link is like a delivery driver saying "I delivered it" with no tracking number. Say: "Give me the proof link."
