## How AI Thinks (Agentic 20%)
1. Idea: Sub-agents (little helpers that split up the work).
   - Plain explanation: For a big job, the helper hires small helpers, each takes one part, then it puts the answers together. That is how 140 ads got built so fast.
   - When to use it today: Any big or repeat job.
   - Exact words to say: "split this into parts and run them at the same time."
2. Idea: Self-check before "done" (self-audit loop).
   - Plain explanation: A good helper checks its own work — or has a second helper check it — before it says finished. That is why the daily check said 78.6% instead of a fake 100%.
   - When to use it today: Before anything goes out the door.
   - Exact words to say: "have a separate helper check this before you call it done."
