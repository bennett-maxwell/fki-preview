If you are patching the same hallway leak twice a year, fighting low pressure, or living with galvanized-era pipes, repiping can be the cheaper path over five years — even when the bid feels large up front.

Rare Breed evaluates pipe material, leak history, pressure, and remodel timing so you are not forced into a full repipe when a sectional repair is honest — and not sold a band-aid when the system is failing.

Repipes pair well with bathroom/kitchen remodels and water heater upgrades. Financing may help spread cost; ask the office for current options.

What is typically included

  • Leak and pressure assessment
  • Sectional vs whole-home recommendation
  • Coordination notes for remodel timing

FAQ

How long does a repipe take?

Depends on home size, access, and material. Many homes are multi-day projects.

Will you open every wall?

Access strategy aims to minimize finish damage; some openings are unavoidable.

Can I repipe just bathrooms?

Sometimes — we say when sectional work is responsible vs kicking the can.

What about water heaters during a repipe?

Often smart to align heater condition with pipe work; we will say if it is optional.
