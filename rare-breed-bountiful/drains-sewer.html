FAQ

Are chemical drain cleaners OK?

They often fail on serious blockages and can damage pipes/fixtures. Call before a third bottle.

Do you camera lines?

Ask when booking — camera inspection is used when the symptom pattern calls for it.

What about tree roots?

Common on older sewer laterals. Diagnosis first, then clean/repair/line options.
