Rare Breed Plumbing, Heating, and Air services water heater problems for homeowners across Bountiful and Northern Utah. We diagnose the unit and the supply conditions that make heaters fail early: sediment, expansion, venting, and gas/electric supply issues.

If replacement is the right call, we walk through capacity, recovery needs, and install scope before work starts. Financing options may be available for larger projects — ask the office when you call.

FAQ

Should I repair or replace?

Age, leak location, and repair cost vs remaining life decide. We explain both paths.

Do you install tankless?

Ask when you call — availability depends on gas line, venting, and home layout.

How long does replacement take?

Many standard tank swaps complete in a single visit; complex venting or code upgrades take longer.

Is financing available?

Yes for many larger jobs — confirm current partners with the office.
