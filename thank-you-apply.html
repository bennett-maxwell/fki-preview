The Process — No Surprises

What happens from here, step by step.

Bennett reviews every application personally. This isn't a funnel — it's a filter. He only takes clients he believes will succeed. Here's how that decision gets made.

1
Within 48 hours

Bennett reviews your application

He looks at your financial position, your goals, your timeline, and your top franchise categories. He's looking for one thing: whether there are 2–3 concepts in our portfolio that genuinely fit your profile. If there are, you move forward. If not, he'll tell you that directly and point you somewhere better.

2
If you're a match

You receive a personalized AI Blueprint

Before any call happens, you get a document built around your profile — your top 3 franchise matches, territory analysis for your market, Item-19 revenue data from those franchisors, and a podcast episode built from your specific answers. This is your starting point, not a pitch.

3
15 minutes

Fit call — confirm or close the loop

A 15-minute Zoom with Bennett. Not a presentation. A conversation to confirm what the Blueprint surfaced, answer your top questions, and decide whether a full Discovery Day makes sense. If it does, Bennett books it. If it doesn't — for any reason on either side — you part ways with clarity and no hard feelings.

4
Discovery Day — if you're both in

Go deep on your top 2–3 concepts

Full-day session covering franchise validation, territory walks with MapKI data, calls with existing franchisees in your matches, and FDD review with our legal partners. You leave knowing exactly what you'd be buying, from whom, and at what terms. No surprises after this point.
