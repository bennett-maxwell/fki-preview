Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept new patients?

Yes — we are happily accepting new patients of all ages. Use the online intake form above and we will confirm within 1 business hour.

What insurance plans do you accept?

We accept most major PPO plans including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, BCBS, MetLife, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, and Guardian. Call us to verify your specific plan.

Do you offer same-day emergency appointments?

Yes. For dental pain, broken teeth, or lost crowns, call (602) 861-0800 immediately — we reserve slots every day for emergencies.

Do you treat children?

Absolutely. We see kids starting at age 1 and have a kid-friendly environment designed to make their visits fun and stress-free.

How much does a new-patient visit cost without insurance?

Our new-patient cleaning, exam, and X-ray package starts at $149. Ask about our in-house membership savings plan.
