## What The Blueprint Focuses On

The first opportunity is speed-to-lead. New inquiries into Origins don't currently get a guaranteed fast reply, and whoever answers first usually wins the conversation. A Speed-to-Lead Agent replies to every new Origins inquiry in under sixty seconds, in your voice, pulling from your CRM and Drive context.

The second opportunity is follow-up that doesn't depend on memory. Warm leads likely cool off because follow-up today is manual. A Follow-Up Nurture Agent runs a structured, multi-touch sequence automatically - it doesn't forget and doesn't get busy, it just keeps the conversation moving until a lead is ready or clearly isn't a fit.

The third opportunity is proposals and admin eating your hours. Admin work costs around fifteen hours a week. A Proposal Drafter turns scope notes into a working first draft, plus an Admin Assistant handles scheduling and status updates.
