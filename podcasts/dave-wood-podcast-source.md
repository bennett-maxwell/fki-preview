## About This Business
Wook Enterprises is a business services company led by Dave Wood. They currently use WordPress, Google Workspace, Social Media and serve business services and consulting.

## The 6 AI Agents Built for Wook Enterprises

### Agent 1: Speed-to-Lead Response Agent
Handles every inbound inquiry within 60 seconds. For business services, this means responding to new business inquiries and managing client relationships. Studies show 78% of customers buy from the first responder (Harvard Business Review, 2023).

### Agent 2: Smart Scheduling and Routing Agent
Routes business operations and advisory requests to the right team member based on availability, expertise, and location. Eliminates the back-and-forth that costs business services businesses 5-8 hours per week.

### Agent 3: Proposal and Quote Draft Agent
Generates professional business operations and advisory proposals in under 2 minutes using the business's actual pricing, past projects, and industry benchmarks. Each proposal is customized to the prospect's specific needs.

### Agent 4: Follow-Up Nurture Agent
Maintains contact with leads who aren't ready to buy yet. Sends personalized follow-ups based on their specific interests and timeline — not generic drip campaigns.

### Agent 5: Review and Reputation Agent
Automatically requests reviews from satisfied customers at the optimal moment. Monitors and alerts on new reviews across Google, Yelp, and industry-specific platforms.

### Agent 6: Analytics and Reporting Agent
Provides a single dashboard showing leads, conversions, revenue attribution, and AI agent performance. Dave Wood sees exactly what's working without pulling reports manually.

## Objection Handling

### "Is this going to replace my team?"
No. These agents handle the repetitive tasks your team shouldn't be doing — data entry, initial responses, follow-up sequences. Your team focuses on the high-value work: closing deals, building relationships, delivering great business operations and advisory.

### "What about the learning curve?"
The 3/7/30 timeline is designed for zero disruption. By day 3, you'll see the agents working. By day 7, your team will wonder how they worked without them.

### "How is this different from other AI tools?"
These aren't generic chatbots. Every agent is built specifically for business services — using your actual services, your pricing, your brand voice. They integrate with WordPress, Google Workspace, Social Media, not replace them.
