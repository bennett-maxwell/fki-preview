SECTION 2 — Where Your Time Leaks Today

Britt, your studio is an intentional boutique. You take a measured number of luxury weddings each year so every couple gets the same small, attentive team. What slows the growth ceiling is not the work itself — it is the operational weight around the work.

Inquiries arrive at all hours. Couples evaluate multiple planners simultaneously. Research shows 78 percent of contracts go to the first vendor to respond. When you are mid-wedding-day or mid-design-review, the inquiry waits four hours, sometimes 24 hours. The couple has already booked someone else by the time you write back.

Vendor coordination is the second drain. Every active wedding has 10 to 15 vendors. Each vendor needs the timeline, the venue layout, the parking instructions, the catering schedule, the day-of contact information. When details change — and they always change — every vendor needs the update. Manually, that is hours of email per week per wedding.

Day-of communication is the third. The run sheet exists. The vendors received it. But on the actual wedding day there are floral substitutions, weather adjustments, family timing shifts, ceremony cue changes. Each one requires a flurry of texts to update vendors in flight. Your lead planner is doing this from her phone while the ceremony is starting.

Post-wedding follow-through is the fourth leak. Without automation, the lifecycle ends when the wedding ends — and you miss the referral pipeline that should be growing your studio for free.

SECTION 3 — The Six AI Agents Built for Your Studio

The first is your Wedding Inquiry Response Agent. When a couple submits an inquiry through your website, your Instagram DM, or a referral, the agent replies within 60 seconds — pulling their name, venue, date, and vision, reflecting it back in your studio's voice, and offering a discovery call. You sleep, the agent works. You have already responded by the time competing planners pour their first coffee.

The second is your Wedding Day Timeline Agent. It builds and maintains the minute-by-minute run sheet for every active wedding. It sends vendor confirmation reminders 72 hours, 24 hours, and 2 hours before arrival. When something changes, you update one place — the agent pushes the update to every vendor who needs to know.

The third is your Client Communication Lifecycle Agent. From the booking confirmation through the anniversary nudge, every couple gets the right milestone message at the right time. Eleven milestones, every couple, on time, automatically. The lifecycle that previously ended at the wedding now compounds your referral pipeline for free.

The fourth is your Studio Performance Agent. It aggregates your bookings, revenue per wedding tier, vendor performance, referral conversion, and inquiry-to-booking ratio into a weekly digest. You see the picture without compiling it manually.

The fifth is your Vendor Coordination Assistant. When a new wedding books, the agent drafts the introduction emails to your recommended vendor partners. When contracts arrive, the agent tracks signature status. You stop being the spreadsheet manager and go back to being the design director.

The sixth is your Wedding Proposal Generator. When a qualified couple needs a tailored proposal, the agent generates a 600-word personalized proposal in under 3 minutes — in your studio's intentional, design-led voice.

SECTION 4 — What AI Does NOT Touch

Your reputation is built on warmth. There is no chatbot on your homepage. Your couples are choosing you because they want intentional design and a small attentive team. AI handles the operational layer beneath the craft — never the craft itself.

AI does not write contracts, run fully automated day-of logistics, or replace the actual design conversation. The wedding day is high-stakes and people-intensive. Your lead planner runs the day. AI prepares the run sheet and tracks the timeline behind the scenes.
