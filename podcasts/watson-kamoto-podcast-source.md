## What The Blueprint Focuses On

The first opportunity is speed to lead. When a production or agency inquiry comes in, the first useful response should happen quickly, with a clear next step and no invented pricing or availability promises.

The second opportunity is project intake. A rough inquiry should become a structured brief with the objective, timeline, production needs, staffing questions, missing assets, and the next action.

The third opportunity is follow-up consistency. Warm opportunities should not depend on memory or manual tracking. An AI follow-up assistant can draft the next message from the latest notes while keeping the tone professional.

## The Three Core Agents

The Speed-to-Lead Response Agent drafts a fast first response, confirms the project type, asks for timeline and desired outcome, and routes the lead into the right next step.

The Project Intake Brief Agent converts a raw inquiry into a usable production brief with objective, audience, timeline, needed assets, staffing questions, and next action.

The Follow-Up and Client Update Agent creates concise follow-up or client-update drafts from the latest notes. It includes completed items, open questions, and the next step without inventing facts.

## Guardrails

The Blueprint should not promise a specific return, claim a fake testimonial, or invent outcomes. It should show Watson the operating leverage clearly, keep all claims grounded, and route every call to action through the tracked qualifier before any booking step opens.
