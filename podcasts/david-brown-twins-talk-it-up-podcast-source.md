## The six AI Employees we built, and what each one gives back

**One — the Speed-to-Lead Employee.** David, you told us your response speed is
next-day. In training and coaching, the buyer is usually scoping several options
in a single sitting, and whoever answers while they are still in that sitting
tends to get the conversation. So we built an AI Employee that answers every new
inquiry in under sixty seconds, in your voice, acknowledges the specific thing
they asked about, asks the two questions that actually decide fit — how many
people would be in the room, and what they want to be different in ninety days —
and then offers two real times from your calendar. The benefit is blunt: you
stop losing leads you already earned to whoever happened to reply faster. At ten
leads a month you cannot afford to lose any of them to a delay.

**Two — the Follow-Up Nurture Employee.** Follow-up was one of the two pressure
points you named, and it is the honest weak spot of every two-person practice,
because follow-up competes directly with delivering the work. We built an AI
Employee that runs a structured four-touch sequence and deliberately varies the
angle each time: a relevant episode of one of your shows, then a leadership or
soft-skills insight aimed at that person's actual role, then a short case
example, then a direct ask. It never repeats an angle, it never nags, and it
stops the instant someone replies. The benefit is that warm leads stop quietly
going cold during the weeks you and Danny are booked solid.

**Three — the Scheduling Employee.** Scheduling was your other named pressure
point, and with two of you it is a real tax. This AI Employee handles the
back-and-forth: it works out whether a request needs one brother or both, it
proposes times that respect what is already booked and the travel around it, it
confirms in writing, and it sends a reminder the day before with everything the
other side needs to arrive prepared. It asks before it double-books either of
you. The benefit is hours a week back, and fewer engagements that slip because a
calendar thread died.

**Four — the Podcast-to-Pipeline Employee.** This is the one we would be most
excited about if we were you. You already produce Twins Talk It Up and Twins
Talk Tech Leadership. That is a standing library of proof that you know what you
are talking about, and right now it is being treated as content rather than as
pipeline. We built an AI Employee that takes an episode transcript and turns it
into three short social posts with real timestamps, a lesson written specifically
for an MSP owner, a two-sentence outreach line that references the episode for a
named prospect, and a sponsor-facing summary. The benefit is that every episode
you were already going to record starts generating conversations, at no extra
production cost and no extra recording time.

**Five — the Channel Partner Reactivation Employee.** Channel Programs and
RevOps Fast Start are real lines in your business, which means you already have
a list of MSP and channel contacts who talked to you once and then went quiet.
That is pipeline you have already paid for. This AI Employee reopens those
conversations by referencing the specific prior discussion, offering one
genuinely useful thing with no strings attached, and asking a single
low-friction question. No "just checking in." And if the record shows someone
asked not to be contacted, it stops cold. The benefit is new conversations at
zero new acquisition cost.

**Six — the Program Proposal Employee.** After a good discovery call there is
still a proposal to write, and a blank page is where deals lose their momentum.
This AI Employee turns your discovery notes into a first-draft program proposal:
the outcome the client asked for, a session-by-session outline drawn from your
real service lines, what the client needs to supply, and a clear next step. It
leaves pricing as a marked fill-in — it will never invent a price or a
guarantee. The benefit is that proposals go out days sooner, because you are
editing something instead of starting one.
