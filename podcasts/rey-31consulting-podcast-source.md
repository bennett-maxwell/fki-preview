## About This Business
31 Consulting is a business consulting company led by Rey. They currently use Slack and serve B2B clients seeking business consulting and operational improvement.

## The 6 AI Agents Built for 31 Consulting

### Agent 1: Speed-to-Lead Response Agent
Handles every inbound inquiry within 60 seconds. For business consulting, this means capturing every inquiry before competitors respond. Studies show 78% of customers buy from the first responder.

### Agent 2: Smart Scheduling and Routing Agent
Routes service requests to the right team member based on availability, expertise, and location. Eliminates the back-and-forth that costs business consulting businesses 5-8 hours per week.

### Agent 3: Proposal and Quote Draft Agent
Generates professional proposals in under 2 minutes using the business actual pricing, past projects, and industry benchmarks.

### Agent 4: Follow-Up Nurture Agent
Maintains contact with leads who are not ready to buy yet. Sends personalized follow-ups based on their specific interests and timeline.

### Agent 5: Review and Reputation Agent
Automatically requests reviews from satisfied customers at the optimal moment. Monitors and alerts on new reviews across Google, Yelp, and industry-specific platforms.

### Agent 6: Analytics and Reporting Agent
Provides a single dashboard showing leads, conversions, revenue attribution, and AI agent performance. Rey sees exactly what is working without pulling reports manually.
