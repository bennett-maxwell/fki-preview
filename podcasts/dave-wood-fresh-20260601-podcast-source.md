SEGMENT 1 - DIRECT OPENING
Dave, the simple frame is this: Mahana Fresh has a fresh fast-casual story, a franchise operating model, and a store-level execution surface where AI can help without changing the guest promise. The opportunity is not to bolt on a giant platform. The opportunity is to remove repeated work from local operators, franchise support, and guest follow-up.

SEGMENT 2 - WHAT THE SOURCES SHOW
The Mahana Fresh franchise page describes support around real estate, design and construction, training, and grand opening support. That points to a franchise system where many tasks repeat across openings and operators. The location page shows a multi-market footprint. That means local execution matters: every store needs clear actions, quick guest recovery, and consistent brand support.

SEGMENT 3 - REVIEWS AND GUEST EXPERIENCE FRAME
This fresh run does not scrape or invent reviews. The review strategy is a workflow recommendation: collect actual reviews, order comments, catering feedback, and manager notes, then summarize repeated themes. The AI should not create public replies without review. It should draft options and surface the operational cause: speed, accuracy, menu clarity, pickup friction, catering follow-up, or guest recovery.

SEGMENT 4 - FIVE-SEGMENT AI OPPORTUNITY MAP
1. Store demand: weekly local actions for catering, partnerships, lapsed guests, and review response.
2. Franchise support: owner-ready checklists for training, opening milestones, and recurring questions.
3. Guest experience: feedback themes and response drafts tied to operational fixes.
4. Content and community: store-specific posts and outreach that add value before asking.
5. Operating proof: a weekly table of actions created, actions completed, and source data used.

SEGMENT 5 - TECH STACK AND DATA
Start with simple inputs: store location, website pages, public location details, review exports, manager notes, catering inquiries, and training documents. Avoid sensitive personal data in the first pilot. Keep every AI output traceable to an input so the operator can trust the result.

SEGMENT 6 - FIRST 3 / 7 / 30 DAYS
Days 1 through 3: pick one pilot store and one workflow. Use store demand because it creates visible value quickly.
Days 4 through 7: review quality, remove noisy outputs, and tighten the next-action format.
Days 8 through 30: expand only after the first workflow is repeatable. Add franchisee enablement or guest experience next.
