## Talk track

Zak, Red Sands Vacation Properties wins when guest questions are answered quickly, owners get clean updates, turnovers are ready before check-in, and the team can see what needs attention. AI should prepare the work so your team can move faster. It should not invent rates, policies, availability, or owner decisions.

The first helper is guest follow-up. It drafts responses for inquiry questions, booking hesitations, stay details, and post-stay follow-up.

The second helper is booking qualification. It matches guests to properties based on group size, dates, trip type, budget notes, and approved property details.

The third helper handles property-owner updates. It turns booking, maintenance, and guest notes into clear owner-ready summaries.

The fourth helper is turnover readiness. It summarizes housekeeping, maintenance, guest requests, access details, and blockers before check-in.

The fifth helper is review and rebooking follow-up. It drafts useful messages after completed stays when no unresolved issue is open.

The sixth helper is operations reporting. It turns bookings, owner activity, guest issues, and team workload into a weekly operating snapshot.
