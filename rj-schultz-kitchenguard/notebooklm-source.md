## 1. Business Overview

RJ Schultz operates a Wisconsin territory under **Kitchen Guard Franchising**, a B2B commercial kitchen exhaust hood cleaning brand. The brand was founded in 2009 as Green Guard Services, rebranded as Kitchen Guard in 2023, and launched national franchising in 2024 under EverSmith Brands (PE-backed by The Riverside Company), HQ San Diego, CA. In just 14 months post-launch, 100+ territories have been awarded. Kitchen Guard's service is NFPA-96 code-mandated — restaurants, hospitals, schools, hotels, stadiums, corporate cafeterias, and assisted living facilities are legally required to have hoods cleaned every 1, 3, 6, or 12 months based on cooking volume.

Brand AUV (Item 19, 2024 FDD) is **$69,000 per franchised location** — low because most units are under one year old. Initial investment: $173,700–$308,550. Franchise fee: $49,000. Leadership includes Tim Breen (Brand President, ex-Subway/Red Lobster) and founder Nathan Leathers.

## 2. RJ's Specific Situation

RJ owns one of the **first Wisconsin territories**. The exact city is not publicly indexed. Crucially, `kitchenguard.com/locations` zip lookup returns **zero results for Wisconsin** — there is no /wisconsin subpage, no local landing page, and no surfaced Google Business Profile for his territory. He is a brand-new operator running on a corporate national funnel that was not designed for per-territory local search dominance.

## 3. Pain Point #1 — Wisconsin SERP Gap (Lead Generation)

Every Milwaukee, Madison, and Green Bay "commercial hood cleaning near me" search today routes to **HOODZ** or independent operators. Corporate's national funnel cannot capture local intent. RJ has no indexed web presence in his own state despite owning the territory. This is the #1 lead-gen bottleneck and the most time-sensitive fix — once another Wisconsin franchisee is awarded, the SERP land-grab gets harder.

## 4. Pain Point #2 — NFPA-96 Re-Book Annuity Leak (Retention)

Hood cleaning is mandated every 1/3/6/12 months by NFPA-96 based on cooking volume. Corporate claims 90%+ client retention, but a $69K AUV across the system means most franchisees are **leaking re-books**. Every missed re-book is $400–$1,200 in lost annual recurring revenue per restaurant. The franchisor does not ship a re-book automation engine — each owner is on their own. Automated SMS + email cadences fired off the cleaning certificate date can lift AUV 25–40% in year two without adding a single new logo.

## 5. Pain Point #3 — After-Hours Inbound Leak (Sales Conversion)

Restaurant general managers contact vendors at **10pm–1am**, after the dinner rush — when no human at a 2-to-5-person territory crew is answering the phone. Every missed after-hours call is a competitor's win. A brand-new Wisconsin territory cannot afford this leak.

## 6. Pain Point #4 — Sub-Scale AUV (Sales Conversion + Retention)

System-wide AUV of $69K implies most territories are not compounding the recurring-revenue model that Kitchen Guard's service mix enables (hood cleaning + filter exchange + Green Steam deep cleaning + repair). The sales cycle is 4–8 touches (compliance officer + facility manager + GM + AP), and corporate ships a generic playbook — not a per-territory sales-ops automation. Filter-exchange upsell alone can double per-account revenue if wired correctly.

## 7. Pain Point #5 — Zero Reputation Flywheel (Reputation)

Brand-new Wisconsin territory = **thin-to-zero Google review profile**. No WI Kitchen Guard GBP found in search. Competitors HOODZ and independent local operators dominate the WI reputation surface. Reputation flywheel has not started. A short-code review request fired on certificate-issuance day can take WI from zero to dominant in 90 days.

## 8. The 5-Agent Opportunity Map

1. **WI Local SEO + GBP Builder** — Builds the kitchen-guard-wisconsin landing page, populates Google Business Profile, seeds first 25 location citations.
2. **After-Hours Quote Agent** — Lives on WI site + Facebook + Instagram DMs. Qualifies inbound by cooking volume, facility type, last-cleaning date. Books on-site quotes 10pm–7am.
3. **NFPA-96 Compliance Packet Generator** — Auto-generates certificate of cleaning + before/after photos + fire-marshal-ready PDF the moment a job closes.
4. **Re-Book Cadence Engine** — Fires SMS + email at day -45, -30, -14, -3 before next mandated cleaning. Books the next visit before the GM has time to call HOODZ.
5. **Wisconsin Reputation Flywheel** — Sends post-job review request from local SMS short code, routes 5-star intent to Google + Facebook, routes 1–4-star to private recovery.

## 9. The 3 Quick Wins

1. **Wisconsin local landing page + GBP** — captures the 5–15 monthly "commercial hood cleaning near me" Milwaukee/Madison/Green Bay searches that route to competitors today.
2. **Automated quarterly/semiannual re-book SMS + email cadence** — lifts AUV 25–40% in year 2 without adding a single new logo.
3. **Inbound lead qualification bot + after-hours capture** — captures 100% of after-hours inbound, books on-site quotes while RJ sleeps.
